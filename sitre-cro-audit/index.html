Prepared by Bhaskar Roy | 2026-05-13 | Period under review: Apr 13 to May 13, 2026 vs prior 30 days Site: sitrestore.com | Market: Denmark and Nordics | Currency: DKK

What's working. Revenue, orders, AOV, and CR are all up on a 21% session increase. The Perfect Duo Bundle is converting at roughly 14.6% from PDP sessions (63 orders on 431 sessions), 4.7x more efficient per session than the solo gel at 3.1%. The press footprint (Grazia, Refinery29, Wonderland, Dazed, ELLE, Glamour) is a real asset most Nordic competitors do not have. Free shipping to EU, UK, and Norway with no stated minimum on EN pages is genuinely generous and unmatched by Peech (free at DKK 600+) or Sinful (DKK 599).

What's leaking.

1. /products/oil-booster returns a 404 today. The Shopify dashboard reports 1,014 sessions to this URL in the last 30 days (+11%). The live product lives at /products/pleasure-boost-oil. At the live PDP's 3.4% CR and 440 DKK price, this redirect alone is worth roughly 11,900 DKK / month in recovered orders. Smallest dev change in this audit, largest single ROI.
2. Danish trust signals and offer mechanics live only on /da. The DA homepage shows "gratis levering ved køb over 299 DKK". The DA Forever Smooth PDP shows "tildelt a-kolben af Forbrugerrådet Tænk", which is the highest chemical safety rating from the Danish Consumer Council. Neither appears on the default English root. Paid Meta traffic in Denmark lands on the EN root and sees the weaker offer.
3. Forever Smooth (290 DKK) is exactly 9 DKK below the Danish 299 DKK free shipping threshold. No progress bar in the cart. The single highest leverage AOV nudge in the entire site sits there unused. A "9 kr to free shipping" bar with a one tap "Add Full Moon Bar 50 g" upsell is a 30-minute build.
4. The cart page on mobile shows "Continue Shopping" ABOVE the product line, and no checkout button above the fold. First paint experience after ATC is a back button suggestion, not a forward step. Cookie banner overlays the button area on the same scroll. This is a checkout rate killer.
5. Two production defects on the second highest traffic PDP. The English Pleasure Boost Oil page (1,014 sessions, 440 DKK) shows the word "besteller" (typo) and "C B D" rendered with forced letter spacing in the hero copy. Trust hit on a premium product where the buyer is already deciding on a regulated ingredient.

Strategic gap. sitre is the only brand in the verified competitor set with no subscribe and save, no quiz, no named clinician, and no published review count per SKU. Peech.dk anchors on 4.9 Trustpilot from 280+ reviews. Foria publishes 1,211 to 2,222 reviews per hero SKU. Kindra cites "6,000+ OB/GYNs". sitre has the editorial press to compete in the category but is missing the three social proof mechanics that close the click to cart gap on a 90% mobile paid funnel.

What the drops are telling us.

  • The 91.6% sessions to ATC drop is mostly a PDP problem. 90% of traffic is paid Meta clicks landing on a cold PDP. The current PDP gives them: a hero image, a benefit bullet list, and an ATC button. No review count next to the title, no Forbrugerrådet Tænk trust mark on the EN PDP, no clinician quote, no sticky mobile ATC. Customers M H, Charlotte, and Thomas are writing the headline copy for sitre inside the reviews module further down the page, and that voice never reaches the hero.

  • The 43.6% ATC to checkout drop is mostly a cart page problem. There is no checkout button above the fold on mobile, no free shipping progress bar, no cross sell. The empty cart state pushes a 10% newsletter signup instead of returning shoppers to product.

  • The 30.9% checkout to completion drop is moderately above the ~25% Shopify mobile median. MobilePay is the dominant Danish wallet (85%+ adoption) and its icon is invisible until the user is already in checkout. Showing it pre checkout would reduce hesitation.
