2 / TL;DR

Three findings drive
the whole roadmap.

01

The business cannot see itself.

Shopify recorded 4,238 orders at a 1.25% conversion rate in the window. GA4 recorded 1 purchase ($39.98). No add to cart, no begin checkout, no purchase events exist, and the configured "key events" are two engagement timers. Every ad platform is optimizing blind.

Tracking repair
02

Paid traffic lands on the worst page.

The best sellers collection takes 65,422 sessions, mostly Facebook, and converts at 0.37%. The homepage converts at 2.01% and the Weekly Catch deal page at 3.21% (Shopify). Lifting that one page to the 1.25% site average is roughly 575 orders per quarter.

Landing experience
03

Mobile paints in 19.4s and the premium is undefended.

68.8% of sessions are mobile and the hero takes 19.4s to paint (LCP), from five verified theme defects. Meanwhile the flagship twin tails cost $89.99 vs $59.99 at Maine Lobster Now, with the justifiers buried below the fold. A one star review says it verbatim: "You have to be kidding $60.00 for a 6oz lobster!"

12 / Voice of customer
● 8,985 reviews, 4.9 stars, 98% recommend

They buy occasions and
arrival day reliability.

The five star corpus centers on two themes: the lobster arrived exactly when promised and still frozen, and it made the occasion (July 4th, anniversaries, "lobster fest at home"). The PDP sells specs. The customers are selling occasions. Copy should follow the customers.

"It arrived on the requested date and was still frozen. I broiled it and it was fantastic. This my 5th or 6th order and never disappointed." Jim L. · 20-24 oz tail
"So worth the waiting anticipation and price. Next time I need to buy 2 lobsters per person at the table." Noelle H. · twin pack tails
● The 95 one star reviews are an objection list
"Opened up the box to find a warm pound of lobster meat and two warm lobster tails. All the ice was gone." Robert P. · cold chain failure
"Did not receive every item I purchased. When I called to be reimbursed I was told that this was not possible." Wendi · fulfillment + service
"You have to be kidding $60.00 for a 6oz lobster!" Shari W. · price shock

Every theme is answerable on page with existing operational facts: the guarantee already exists at checkout, the dry ice packaging already exists in the box, the weights already exist on the scale. None of it is visible at the point of sale.
