High value cards

No value based fee on cards worth $10,000 or less. Above that, a 2.5% fee applies to the value over $10,000 only, paid when you book your grading date. Shipping insurance is 1% of the card's value on cards worth more than $500, charged when you book.

Your grading date

Every card is graded on the date you book. The earliest date is 30 business days out for Glass and oversized cards, and 60 for Poly cards and booklets. Pick any day from there.
