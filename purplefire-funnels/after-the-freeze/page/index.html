    Your first split test is on us. You pay after the first win.

    1. We research the test, design it and build it on a duplicate theme. You approve the design before it launches.

    2. A win is a variant that lifts revenue by at least 5 percent over 7 days at 88 percent significance or higher. Only then does the winning variant go live for all your traffic.

    3. No card on file until the first winner. On the call we tell you what you pay after the first win, and the terms come in writing.

    We're in a code freeze. What do you need from us before December?
    Nothing. Booking touches nothing on your store. The first thing we ask for is five numbers for a December readout, two days before your call, in a form. Planning calls for a January week start on Thu 3 Dec.
    What access do you need, and when?
    For a December readout: five numbers in a form, or GA4 viewer access if you'd rather. No store login and no theme access. For a January launch week: nothing before the planning call. If we take you on, a Slack channel, access to your store and testing tool, and one person who approves designs, from the day the terms are signed. The build goes on a duplicate theme.
    What does it cost?
    The readout and the planning call are free. Your first split test is on us. You pay after the first win. No card on file until then. On the call we tell you what you pay after the first win, and the terms come in writing.
    What does the readout cover?
    Your five Black Friday numbers (sessions, orders, conversion rate, average order value and your top landing page by revenue) beside last year's, where visitors dropped off, and the first three A/B tests we'd run in January, each tied to a number in your readout. It lands in your inbox the day before, so the 15 minutes go on your questions.
    If I pick January, what happens to my first test?
    It is the same first test, and it is still on us. Before the planning call we send the five tests we'd run first on your store, and on the call we pick one together. If we take you on, we design it and build it on a duplicate theme in December or from Mon 4 Jan, and it launches in the week you picked. If it wins, the winning variant goes live and the first invoice follows.
