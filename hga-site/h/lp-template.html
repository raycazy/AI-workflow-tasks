Before you claim

The honest terms, in plain words.

Five things collectors ask before they buy. No fine print below this.

  1. 01

    Will HGA still be around in ten years?

    HGA is owned by UCollect, which acquired it in 2025 and rebuilt it: a published grading standard, new glass and poly housings, and a chip in every label that ties the slab to its permanent record.

  2. 02

    Will the glass scratch?

    The glass resists scratches, which is the point for a card that lives in a cabinet you open every day. It seals like a vacuum and stacks with magnets.

  3. 03

    When is my card graded?

    On the day you choose. From Jan 18 you book an open day on the grading calendar and ship your cards any time before it. Grading starts Feb 1, and your portal shows each step after that. Presale purchases are final and stay on your account until you book.

  4. 04

    Does UCollect's ownership affect my grade?

    No. Every card is graded blind to the same written standard, and the grader never knows whose card it is. HGA grades cards. It does not buy, sell or own them.

  5. 05

    Does $200 cover any card?

    $200 is per standard size card. Booklets, folds and decks are booked separately at $10 per fold. Cards over $10,000 carry a 2.5% fee on the value over $10,000, paid when you book your grading day.
