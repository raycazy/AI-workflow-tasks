The HGA grading system

Four subgrades. Every threshold in writing.

Most grades arrive as one number. Ours arrive as four, each measured against a standard you can read today, and each printed on the back of the label.

01 / CENTERING

Measured,
not eyeballed.

A digital tool measures the borders front and back. Graders confirm by hand.

All 48 answers
What exactly am I buying?

A pass: prepaid grading for one card at our 15 business day tier, in the housing you choose. Every subgrade, the Grade Report and the case are included. Your passes live on your account.

How do I use a pass?

From February 1 the grading calendar opens. Reserve an open day up to a year ahead, assign one pass per card, and ship your cards to arrive that day.

Do passes expire?

No. A pass holds its value until you use it, and you choose the day.

What can I send?

Any sports card or TCG card, any size. Poly has a case for every size on the market, vintage to booklets, and if yours doesn't exist yet, we make it. Glass is offered for every size too.

What about cards worth over $10,000?

No upcharge up to $10,000 in card value. Above that, insurance handling is $200 per $10,000 of value, paid when you reserve. The pass still covers the grade.

Can I give a pass as a gift?

Yes. Mark the order as a gift at checkout, or send any unused pass from My Passes by email.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. If you change your mind, transfer the pass to another account from My Passes.
