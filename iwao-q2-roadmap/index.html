What Q2 proved

All three winners say the same thing about this buyer.

For an 8.000 to 125.000 DKK considered purchase, buyers convert on trust, access to a human, and de-risking, not urgency and price cuts. Q2 removed friction on the page. Q3 should guide the long high ticket decision and capture the buyer who is not ready to buy today. That points at three pillars.

T08 Homepage · PDP Pillar A · Human reassurance Impact High Ease Medium

Showroom visit as a first class conversion

IWAO | A/B | HP + PDP | Book a free showroom visit or virtual demo
Observation
  • The Glostrup showroom and egne teknikere are the single biggest differentiator versus online only competitors.
  • Yet "SHOWROOM" lives as a secondary outlined button with no booking flow and no tracked intent.
  • For an 8.000 to 125.000 DKK purchase, a large share of buyers want to sit in the chair before paying.
Hypothesis

IF we turn booking a showroom visit or virtual demo into a prominent calendar based micro conversion, WE EXPECT more captured high intent leads and more assisted high AOV sales, BECAUSE try before you buy removes the biggest fear on a considered purchase and gives sales a warm, high closing offline channel.

What we are testing
  1. Booking widget: date and time, phone, product of interest.
  2. Homepage hero secondary CTA + PDP entry below ATC.
  3. Variant B adds an exit intent showroom invite.

T09 PDP · Cart Pillar A · Human reassurance Impact High Ease Medium

Scale human contact beyond the phone number

IWAO | A/B | PDP + Cart | Tal med en ekspert: chat, callback, WhatsApp
Observation
  • A static phone number drove +38% ATC in T01, the strongest lever found so far.
  • But a phone call is high friction for many shoppers.
  • There is currently no live chat, callback request, or messaging option anywhere on the site.
Hypothesis

IF we add a "Tal med en ekspert" cluster (live chat, request a callback, WhatsApp) anchored at PDP and cart, WE EXPECT higher ATC and cart to checkout, BECAUSE the winning Q2 lever was human access, and lower friction ways to ask a question capture the buyers who will not pick up the phone.

What we are testing
  1. Contact cluster on PDP and cart.
  2. Proactive chat trigger after dwell on the price section.
  3. Callback request form for after hours.

T10 PDP · Collection Pillar B · De-risk Impact High Ease Medium

Financing first price + Anyday calculator

IWAO | A/B | PDP + PLP | Lead with the monthly figure, not the sticker
Observation
  • Financing ("fra X kr/md med Anyday, rentefri") recurs in the winning trust messaging.
  • The cart and PDP notes flag that financing visibility disappears down funnel: PDP shows 292 kr/md, cart shows a lump 8.495 DKK.
  • A 45.995 kr sticker triggers shock. "292 kr/md" reframes it as affordable.
Hypothesis

IF we lead with the monthly figure as a co primary price anchor on PDP and every collection card, with an interactive "beregn din ydelse" calculator, WE EXPECT higher ATC and PDP click through, BECAUSE monthly framing lowers perceived cost and 0% financing is a decisive de risker on high ticket considered buys.

What we are testing
  1. PDP price block: total + bold monthly + calculator.
  2. Collection cards show "fra X kr/md".
  3. Anyday 0% terms shown inline at the anchor.

T11 PDP · Sitewide Pillar B · De-risk Impact Medium Ease High · fastest win

Replace the "sale ends" countdown with price confidence

IWAO | A/B | PDP | Kill artificial urgency on a premium product
Observation
  • PDPs run a green "TILBUDDET UDLØBER OM..." countdown.
  • This is exactly the promotional, sale site signal that lost in T02 and contradicts the brand's winning premium positioning.
  • Artificial urgency can erode trust on a 50.000 kr purchase.
Hypothesis

IF we replace the countdown with a price confidence frame (PriceRunner købsgaranti, nettets laveste pris, lowest price guarantee), WE EXPECT equal or higher ATC with higher trust, BECAUSE T02 already proved this audience converts on legitimacy not promo pressure, and price match confidence removes the "should I wait or shop around" objection without sale site theatrics.

What we are testing
  1. A: countdown (control).
  2. B: price guarantee confidence frame.
  3. C: no timer at all.

T12 PDP · Sitewide Pillar B · De-risk Impact High Ease Medium

Risk reversal as a hero, not a footnote

IWAO | A/B | PDP | Headline the trial, return, and own technicians
Observation
  • "Risk exposure" is a named buyer barrier in the pipeline.
  • Strong guarantees exist (14 dages garanti, 24 to 36 mdr. reklamationsret, gratis levering, egne teknikere, PriceRunner up to 50.000 kr) but they sit low on the page.
  • High ticket leaders make the trial or return promise a headline.
Hypothesis

IF we elevate one bold risk reversal promise next to the ATC ("14 dage til at prøve den. Vi henter den gratis, hvis du ikke er tilfreds. Egne teknikere leverer og installerer."), WE EXPECT higher ATC, BECAUSE the fear of being stuck with the wrong 8.000 to 125.000 DKK chair is the dominant objection, and a visible plain language safety net neutralises it at the decision point.

What we are testing
  1. PDP risk reversal strip directly near the ATC button.
  2. Plain language, one bold promise, not a badge row.
  3. If operations allow, test an explicit home trial promise.

T13 Homepage · Sitewide Pillar C · Guide & capture Impact High Ease Medium · asset exists

Make FIND MIN MASSAGESTOL the primary guided selling entry

IWAO | A/B | HP | Surface the quiz that is already buried in the header
Observation
  • Buyers struggle to distinguish 2D / 3D / 4D / 8D and which model fits ("model confusion" and "fit uncertainty" barriers).
  • The brand already has a "FIND MIN MASSAGESTOL" quiz, but it is a small header button competing with the showroom CTA.
  • The asset exists and is underused.
Hypothesis

IF we promote the quiz to the homepage hero primary CTA and add it as a PDP and collection "ikke sikker på hvilken model?" entry plus exit intent, WE EXPECT more qualified PDP visits and higher ATC, BECAUSE guided selling routes confused high ticket buyers to the right chair instead of leaving them to scan 16 products blindly.

What we are testing
  1. HP hero quiz CTA vs the current generic CTA.
  2. PDP and collection "not sure which model?" entry.
  3. Exit intent quiz prompt.

Persistent compare tool + 2D / 3D / 4D / 8D explainer

IWAO | A/B | PLP + PDP | Keep the comparison on site, stop the leak to Google
Observation
  • "No comparison tool causing buyers to leave and Google to compare" is an explicitly named collection page leak.
  • A standalone, sitewide compare drawer plus a simple tech tier explainer attacks "model confusion" directly.
  • It stops buyers leaving the site to research elsewhere.

IF we add a floating "sammenlign" tool (up to 3 chairs, spec + price + monthly side by side) plus a short 2D / 3D / 4D / 8D explainer, WE EXPECT higher PDP click through and ATC and lower exit to search, BECAUSE keeping the comparison on site removes the reason to leave and answers "what is the difference?" at the moment of doubt.

What we are testing
  1. Floating compare drawer (up to 3 chairs).

T15 Sitewide Pillar C · Guide & capture Impact High Ease Medium

Capture the long deliberation

IWAO | A/B | Sitewide | Exit intent lead magnet + save and price alert
Observation
  • ATC sits at ~2.5%, so roughly 97.5% of high intent traffic leaves with no trackable next step.
  • An 8.000 to 125.000 DKK purchase has a long, multi session deliberation.
  • There is no on site capture for the not ready buyer beyond a newsletter.
Hypothesis

IF we add an exit intent or scroll depth capture offering a buyer's guide, a financing pre check, or a showroom invite, plus a "gem / få prisbesked" save option, WE EXPECT more captured leads and more returning visit conversions, BECAUSE long deliberation needs a low commitment next step, and owned contact lets email and retargeting carry the buyer back.

What we are testing
  1. Exit intent modal variants: guide vs showroom vs financing.
  2. "Gem / få prisbesked" save and price drop alert.
  3. Winning offer feeds the email flows.
