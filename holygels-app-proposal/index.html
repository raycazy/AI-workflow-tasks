What is the problem?

A customer picks her designs weeks before her box ships. In that time a design can sell out, and Recharge has no automatic swap for the designs inside a bundle. Your team can only swap them in bulk by hand.

She picks 4 designs One sells out Three things go wrong

  • New orders

    A new order with a sold out design usually does not split into its designs, so your team splits or swaps it by hand.

    23 orders since 1 Nov held a design already off the store. As of 20 Nov

  • Renewals

    On charge day Recharge stops the order with an inventory error and tries again every day until the design is back in stock or she changes it. Until then there is no box for her and no revenue for you. Five designs show more ordered than in stock today; we have not confirmed that renewals caused it.

    470 blocked on stock from 7 to 20 Nov, worth £17,251; 17 of them are still blocked. As of 21 Nov

    An empty box gets the bundle's default design: Aloha on the 2, 3, 4 and 6 Set Bundles, and Aloha is sold out. Refill has no default. Recharge, 23 Nov

  • The customer

    No warning when a design sells out. She only hears on charge day, when Recharge sends a general out of stock email that says a bestseller may be sent instead, which nothing does automatically. Recharge and Klaviyo, 23 Nov

What are we fixing?

Two fixes are already live on the site. The app closes what the site cannot reach, and the emails keep the customer in the loop.

Live since 19 Nov

  • Sold out designs can no longer be picked, and a cart that still holds one is stopped before checkout.

  • Refill orders that Recharge refused to split now split. This was the cause behind 24 of 26 such orders. As of 20 Nov

What the app adds

On 18 Nov we said no app yet. Since then we found orders that skip the site, and that half of the next two weeks' renewals hold a sold out design. So we now propose extending the small app already installed for the checkout timer. Nothing new to install.

A

Checkout rule Shopify checkout

Closes: orders that skip the site

Built to stop an order with a sold out design at payment, including express checkouts like Shop Pay and Apple Pay, and links in checkout emails. We test each before it goes live.

B

Renewal swap Recharge, every day

Closes: subscriptions already holding one

Before renewals charge, it swaps a sold out design on the subscription for an in stock one, using the rule you pick.

C

Customer emails Klaviyo, when the app swaps

She hears before the charge: old design, new design, a link to change it. Later, a back in stock email when her design returns.

D

Daily summary Your team's inbox

Each morning: swaps made, renewals that need a person, designs to restock first.

You choose the swap rule

Reply with your pick, or an order of two.

  • Closest colour: a blue for Aloha
  • Most stock: the most units left
  • Bestseller: the top seller in stock
  • Your own list: your picks, in order
  • Her earlier picks: one she chose before

Whatever rule you pick

  • Never a design already in her box.

  • Nails stay nails, toes stay toes.

  • Only designs that bundle allows.

  • Picks spread out and checked against units left.

97%of affected subscriptions (1,582 of 1,637) share a tag, almost always a colour, with an in stock design. Stock and bundle limits are checked before any swap, so some will still go to your team. As of 21 Nov

What the customer hears today

  • One Klaviyo reminder about 58 hours before the charge. It changes with her order count, never with what is in her box.

  • No warning when a design sells out. On charge day, when the charge fails, Recharge sends its own out of stock email ("Action required: A shade in your next box is out of stock"), at most once a week while the charge keeps failing. It says a bestseller may be sent instead, which nothing does automatically.

  • Recharge retries the charge every day, for up to its recovery window (120 days by default), until the design is back or she changes it.

  • Card failures get Recharge's payment recovery emails. If every retry fails, Recharge cancels her and Klaviyo's cancellation emails go out.

  • Nothing confirms a design change.

We can improve these emails now, before the app. Recharge and Klaviyo, read 23 Nov

What we need from you

Four answers, and we start the dry run.

  • Which swap rule?

    One rule, or an order of two, for example closest colour first, then most stock.

  • Do the email wordings work for you?

    We send you drafts after the dry run.

  • Who gets the daily summary, and what replaces a design with no match?

    For example White French Tip Toes, which has no colour match in stock.

  • Can customers turn off automatic swaps?

    If yes, they get the "pick a new design" email instead.
