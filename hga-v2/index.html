Finally

A new system that grades in plain sight.

Yeah, we know. HGA has been gone for a while.

You have every reason to be skeptical of our return. We came back because we saw a troubling trend in the card grading industry, and we wanted to do something about it.

For far too long, collectors like you have sent off their most valuable cards and simply hoped for the best. Meanwhile, you're met with hidden grading processes, uncertain turnaround times, and little explanation behind your final grade.

That's because the industry is missing the most important thing: transparency.

You shouldn't be asked for blind trust while being kept in the dark. You don't need more promises. You need more proof.

And what better way to give you proof than to show you everything?

That's why, for the first time ever, HGA (now powered by uCollect) lets you visually follow your card from delivery to grade reveal. You're never left in the dark about what's happening to your card, or wondering when you'll finally get your grade.

What is a Founder's pass?

Prepaid grading for one card at the 15 business day tier, in the housing you choose, at Founder's pricing. Your passes live on your account and never expire. Up to 100 per housing per order, with no limit on orders.

When can I send my cards?

HGA officially reopens February 1, 2027. From then, reserve a grading day up to a year ahead and ship your cards to arrive that day. The 15 business day clock starts at carrier delivery.

What is Glass Box Grading™?

HGA's new grading experience: AI assisted grading, card tracking, detailed grading reasons, digital verification and a guaranteed turnaround, all visible to you through uCollect.

What can I send?

Any sports card or TCG card, any size. HGA Poly has a case for every size on the market, vintage to booklets, and if yours doesn't exist yet, we make it. HGA Glass is offered for every size too.

What about cards worth over $10,000?

There is no card value upcharge through $10,000. Above that, insurance handling is $200 per $10,000 of value, paid when you reserve. The pass still covers the grade.

Can I give a pass as a gift?

Yes. Mark your order as a gift at checkout, or send any unused pass from My Passes by email.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. If you change your mind, transfer the pass to another account from My Passes.
