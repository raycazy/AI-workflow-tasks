Funnel 3Named stores: one short video each

Prototype for PurpleFire

Strategy 3MessageAssets

15 named stores, one short video each

You send 15 founders a short video about one thing on their own store and ask for a reply, not a call.

The idea

You send 15 founders a video of 40 to 60 seconds about one thing on their own store, with the past A/B test that matches it. Each store is named below with the reason it was picked and the person to write to. Nothing is built or hosted.

Hypothesis

Founders may not book a call now because of Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the holidays, so the message asks only for a reply and a January start. It is wrong if the 15 stores send zero positive replies (a yes or a question) by Fri 18 Dec.

The 15 stores

Twelve stores are ready from Wed 2 Dec. Two wait for a legal read (Durston Gear and GORNATION), and one needs your call first: C4 Fabrication. The reason is on its card.

The private sheet has a LinkedIn profile for 13 of the 15 contacts (found in search, not opened) and an email for 12 stores. How the stores were chosen: each is similar to a brand PurpleFire has run A/B tests for, runs on Shopify, is led by its founder or owner (Gossamer Gear by a CEO), has no A/B test seen running on the site, has a named person to write to, and has a gap on the page that a past winning test addressed.

Seven spare stores are in the same sheet, in case one of these 15 is a no.

Cost

No media spend. Your time is about 10 minutes per video, so about 2.5 hours for all 15. Est.

Message

Three short messages per store: Day 1, Day 3 and Day 7. Fill the slots from that store's card in Assets.

Who, to whom and when

Who sends
You, from your own inbox or your LinkedIn.
To whom
The contact named in each row of the 15 stores on Strategy 3.
When
Day 1 can be any working day from Wed 2 Dec 2026, after Cyber Week; nothing goes out in Black Friday week. Send all 15 Day 1 messages by Fri 11 Dec, so every Day 7 lands before the read on Fri 18 Dec.

Nothing goes to a contact in Germany or Canada (GORNATION, Durston Gear) until a legal read confirms a first message is allowed there. No Danish store is on the list. Rules for the UK and the US were not checked either; the same legal read can cover them.

The three messages

Day 3 is two days after Day 1 and Day 7 is six days after it; a send that falls on a weekend moves to the Monday. Three days in a row is too pushy for someone who has never heard of you.

How to use: the same text works as an email or a LinkedIn message. On LinkedIn, leave out the subject line and send it as a message or an InMail, never inside a connection request. Day 3 and Day 7 go in the same thread, only to people who have not replied.

Fill from the card: the one thing is point 1 and the past test and result is point 2, both in your own words. Name the past client only if their result is already on your own site or they have said yes. Otherwise say what kind of store it was. Attach the still from the video: their page on screen at the moment you name the one thing.

Fill from the card: the change on your page is point 3, the line that says what the change would be on their page.

After Day 7: nobody writes again unless they reply.

When they reply

Answer the same working day. A yes gets three 15 minute times in January.

On this tab

Assets

One card per store, in the same order and with the same numbers as the table on Strategy 3. Each card holds the points to cover in that store's video.

How to record

  • Keep it to 40 to 60 seconds with their page on screen, at phone width where the gap is on a phone.
  • Cover the points on the card in order.
  • Name the past client only if their result is already on your own site or they have said yes. Otherwise say what kind of store it was.
  • Never promise a result on their store.
