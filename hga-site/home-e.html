FAQ

Quick answers to what collectors ask most.

When can I book my grading date?

Booking opens January 18, 2027. We email you an invite to book, and the calendar shows the next available grading date. Grading begins February 1, 2027.

When do I send my cards?

Any time before your booked grading date. Your confirmation page tells you when.

What's the turnaround?

Every presale grade, Poly and Glass, is back within 30 days of your grading date.

What if an earlier date opens up?

Choose "move me up automatically" when you book, and we'll move you to an earlier date if one opens.

Does the presale cover booklets and decks?

Presale slots cover standard size cards. Booklets, folds and decks are booked separately.

Can I transfer my presale slot?

No. Presale purchases can't be transferred.

What if I need a card graded sooner?

Standard grading is available to buy separately.
