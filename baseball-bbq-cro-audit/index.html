4 / Test 01

Test 01: Featured Products collection rebuild

Why this test /collections/home-page-featured-products takes 783 sessions over 90 days at a 37% bounce rate (people stay and browse) and produces zero purchases. This is the most-clicked collection in the catalog and the most directly broken commercial surface on the site. Permanent fix, not seasonal.
Hypothesis Re-curating the collection to lead with bundles and demoting zero-CR SKUs will convert 1 to 3% of the 783 sessions into purchases, turning $0 into $500 to $1,500 / month.
Control
  • Mixed-priority SKUs in arbitrary order
  • No bundle prioritization
  • No "Most Picked" or "Best Value" badges
  • Same "76 reviews" on every card
  • No "Build your own" CTA
  • Sessions: 783 / 90d · Purchases: 0 · Revenue: $0
Variant
  • Row 1: 3 hero bundles — Triple Play ($63), Grand Slam ($117, "Most Picked"), MVP ($177)
  • Row 2: Top 6 single tools by review count
  • Row 3: "Build your own" CTA → /collections/customizable
  • Demote: any SKU with zero sales in last 90d
  • Add: Judge.me per-product stars on cards
  • Target CR: 1.5 to 3% · Target / mo: $500 to $1,500

Primary metric: Purchases originating from /collections/home-page-featured-products · Secondary: collection to PDP CTR, bundle CTR, bounce. Sample: 261 sessions / mo baseline. 6 to 8 weeks to detect a 1 to 3 purchase / mo lift at 90% confidence.

5 / Test 02

Test 02: PDP buy-box rebuild

Why this test PDP to Add-to-Cart is 1.20%. Industry benchmark for branded ecom is 4 to 7%. 9,059 PDP views in 28 days yield only 109 carts. The brand's actual trust assets (4.9/2,847 reviews, GMA press, lifetime warranty, free engraving, MLB license) are not in the buy box. The current badges are generic.
Hypothesis Replacing generic trust badges with brand-specific reassurance, surfacing star rating above the title, and inlining the engraving upsell will lift PDP to ATC from 1.20% to 1.80 to 2.40%. At a $131 AOV, that is +25 to +50 incremental purchases / 28d, +$3.3k to +$6.5k / month.
Control
  • Star rating below the fold (scroll to find)
  • Badges: "Top Quality / Fast Ship / Support / SSL" (generic)
  • "76 reviews" with no visible star count
  • Customization sent to a separate page
  • No "What's included" image on sets
  • PDP to ATC: 1.20% · 9,059 views, 109 carts
Variant
  • 4.9 stars + 76 reviews immediately under product title
  • Badges: lifetime warranty, free engraving, ships 1-2 days, free ship $100+
  • "+ Add custom engraving (+$10 / +$20 with logo)" inline checkbox
  • "What's included" labeled hero image on bundle PDPs
  • "As Seen on GMA" strip below buy box
  • Target PDP to ATC: 1.80 to 2.40%

Primary metric: PDP to Add-to-Cart rate · Secondary: time on PDP, scroll depth past buy box, engraving attach rate. Sample: Aggregate across top 5 PDPs (~2,000 PDP views / 28d). Signal in 3 to 4 weeks.

6 / Test 03

Test 03: Gift-Finder Quiz

Why this test 2,085 product variants and zero decision aid. The primary buyer is a gift-giver (mom, partner, kid buying for dad / grandpa / brother). Choice paralysis is the structural enemy. A quiz that returns "Recommended for him: Grand Slam Set" collapses the decision and creates a defensible feature competitors do not have.
Hypothesis A 6-question quiz returning a personalized bundle recommendation will lift session CR for quiz-takers to 3 to 6% (vs sitewide 0.80%), plus build a permanent email retargeting asset.
Control (today)
  • Visitor lands, scrolls a 2,085-variant grid
  • No "Best for Dad" filter, no quiz, no comparison view
  • Picks based on familiarity, team, or bounces
  • Customization buried under a separate URL
  • Sitewide CR: 0.80% · PDP to ATC: 1.20%
Variant
  • Q1: Who is it for? (Dad / Grandpa / Brother / Self / Other)
  • Q2: Favorite team? (MLB picker)
  • Q3: How often does he grill? (Weekend warrior / Tailgater / Pro)
  • Q4: Engrave text or logo?
  • Q5: Budget tier? ($50 / $100 / $200+)
  • Q6: Gift now or later?
  • Result: pre-customized bundle. Email capture pre-result.
  • Target quiz CR: 3 to 6% · Email retargeting asset

Primary metric: Quiz-completer purchase rate vs non-quiz session CR · Secondary: quiz starts, completions, email opt-in rate, time-to-purchase, AOV. Target: 500 completions / mo, ~8 weeks to compare cohorts.

16 / Market gap

Defend the premium niche.
Do not compete on price.

● Where Baseball BBQ wins
  • Patented bat-handle design. YouTheFan and MLB Shop sell generic stainless tools with team logos riveted on. The IP is defensible.
  • MLB + MiLB + College SKU coverage at one site. Rare combo in the category.
  • Laser engraving in 1 to 2 business days. Competitors either don't offer it or take 7 to 14 days.
  • Lifetime warranty. Most competitors offer no warranty.
  • GMA / Live with Kelly / Fox News press. Generic competitors do not have this validation.
● Where Baseball BBQ loses
  • Retail distribution. YouTheFan is in Home Depot, Walmart, Dick's. Casual gift shoppers find them in store first.
  • Price floor. GEBALA at $15-30 and YouTheFan at $35-50 capture the entry-level gifter.
  • Team-name SEO. "Yankees grill set" returns MLB Shop and YouTheFan first.
  • No subscription / replenishment offer. Wider category gap, but a future moat.
Positioning Statement The premium MLB-licensed grilling gift that arrives engraved in 48 hours. The dad who gets this will cry.
