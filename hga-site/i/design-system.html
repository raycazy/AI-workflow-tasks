The HGA design system.

Dark, exact, and built around the slab. Every color, type style and component on this page is measured from the current build of the HGA site, so anyone building a page, email or lander for HGA can match what is shipping.

Source: hybridgradingapproach.com build, version I Captured: 2026-10-30 Method: computed CSS, Playwright
Brand essence

The future of card grading is here.

HGA grades blind to a published standard, explains every grade with four subgrades, books a grading date and returns every presale grade within 30 days of it, and houses the card in metal and glass. Glass carries the positive story (preserve it, display it, pass it down). Transparency carries the relief story (no more guessing where the card is or why it got its grade).

It is

  • Dark and low key. Near black pages, white type, one red.
  • Glass first. Poly is available but quieter.
  • Exact. Never a superlative without the mechanism behind it.
  • Hobby fluent. Pull, PC, raw, slab, grail, booklet.
  • Empowering. "The moment you've been waiting for."

It is not

  • Light themed. The old Paper and Poppins look is retired.
  • Counting business days. No business day promises, no daily late credit.
  • Crypto flavoured. No NFT or NFC. Lead with RFID, the chip in the label.
  • Marketplace talk. No UCollect marketplace, Club U or custom labels.
  • Loud. No emoji, no exclamation stacks, no shouting headlines.

Logo

Logo usage

Four white SVG lockups from the client plus the UCollect mark. The site is dark only, so the logos are only ever white. The header pairs the HGA wordmark with a small "Powered by UCollect" tag.

Files: assets/hybrid_grading/hga-*.svg and assets/ucollect.svg in the site repo. All fills are #FFFFFF. The viewBox widths are 1000 (HGA, lockup, Glass) and 903 (Poly), so size by height.

Do
  • Use white on the page black, card black or a photo with a dark area behind the mark.
  • Pair with "Powered by UCollect" in the header and footer.
  • Use the Glass and Poly marks as product names in pricing and tier cards.
  • If a light surface is unavoidable (print, email on white), request a dark version of the mark from the client. filter: invert(1) is a stopgap only.
Do not
  • Recolor the mark red or grey, or add a glow or shadow to it.
  • Write "Glass pass" or "Poly pass". The housing name stands alone: "Claim Your Glass", "1 × HGA Glass".
  • Set the UCollect mark larger than the HGA mark on HGA pages.
  • Place the marks on red fills.
Iconography

Icons

One inline SVG sprite on every page: 47 plain stroke glyphs on a 24 unit grid, no fills, currentColor at stroke 2 with round caps and joins. The symbol takes its color from the text around it, so the same glyph is coral inline, white in a list and red inside a square.

The full set (47)

Sizes

In context

Do
  • Reference the sprite: <svg class="ico"><use href="#i-chip"/></svg>. Add new symbols to the sprite, not as inline paths.
  • Keep stroke 2 at 16 to 24px. Go to 1.6 at 20px in the hero points, 1.7 inside the red square.
  • Pair one icon with one short line. The icon names the idea, the text explains it.
  • Use check for yes and x for no; the table and the comparison copy depend on that pairing.
Do not
  • Use emoji, filled icon sets, or two line weights on one page.
  • Draw icons inside a slab outline or any container other than the three above.
  • Build grids of icon + title + text cards. Feature sections are image led.
  • Recolor to grey. Icons are coral, red, green or white.

System

Voice & copy

The voice is calm and exact, and it is transparent about how grading works. The client's house rules: never a superlative without the mechanism behind it, never a number we would not publish, never a promise before it is deliverable. Headlines are either short sentences with a full stop or the approved client copy in Title Case; never mix the two on one page. Lines below are verbatim from the build.

The future of card grading is here.homepage h1
Claim Your Glass. Save $400.offer h2

"Four subgrades on the label, so I can see exactly why it got the grade it did."Danielle R., reviews strip, owner unconfirmed
"My best cards finally look like they belong on a shelf."Chris M., reviews strip, owner unconfirmed
"Finally something better than plastic."Ryan G., reviews strip, owner unconfirmed
Say
  • Pass, never slot. "Claim Your Glass", "your presale pass".
  • Glass and Poly stand alone as housing names. "1 × HGA Glass", not "Glass pass".
  • RFID, "the chip in the label", "tap, open the record".
  • A grading date you book. Cards back within 30 days of that date.
  • Save $400 for Glass. Up to 80% off is true for Poly only.
  • Presale, one word, in running copy. The announcement bar and hero eyebrow set "Pre sale opens Nov 23" as written. Reviews as first name plus last initial.
  • Hobby language: pull, PC, raw, slab, grail, booklet, penny sleeve, toploader.
Never
  • Business day promises (15 business days or any other count), "guaranteed", a daily late credit.
  • NFT, NFC, "patented", UV protection, impact resistant. Lead with RFID, not blockchain; the grading standards page still says "Blockchain record" and whether that stays is open with the client.
  • UCollect marketplace, Club U, custom labels, resale value claims.
  • "Glass box", "black box", "slot".
  • Em dashes, en dashes or hyphenated compounds in new copy written for HGA. Approved client copy keeps its punctuation as written.
  • Exclamation stacks, three word fragment chains, anticipating objections.
  • Emoji, in copy or as icons.
