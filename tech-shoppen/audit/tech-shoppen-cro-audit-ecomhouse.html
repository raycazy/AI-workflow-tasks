Methodology
How this audit was built
Every finding is grounded in GA4 data, live page behaviour, or the customer's own words. Absolute GA4 numbers are directional (consent and the Shopify Web Pixels Manager suppress some events, and a third of purchases arrive with no source), but the step to step ratios are unaffected and they drive every priority in this deck.
// 01
GA4 evidence
90-day pull + sequential funnel from property 375103003: sessions, revenue, device split, per-product landing performance, and a true View to Purchase funnel (Funnel API, not raw event counts).
// 02
Live page & speed
Full walk of homepage, collection, KidsBuddy PDP and cart on desktop and mobile, plus Google PageSpeed on homepage and PDP, plus a tag audit (GTM, GA4, Google Ads, Meta all verified live).
// 03
VOC + competitors
404 Trustpilot reviews (3.9/5) theme coded with verbatim Danish quotes, plus 3 verified competitors: xplora.dk, kidsbuddy.dk and techbutik.dk, each checked live.
One gap worth naming: no behavioral analytics tool (Microsoft Clarity or Hotjar) is installed, so there are no session recordings or rage-click maps behind this audit. Clarity is free; installing it is itself a recommendation of this deck.

02
Page by page
The conversion walk
Why 92.8% of viewers do not add to cart, and why half of mobile checkouts die. Homepage to collection to PDP to cart, every claim verified against a captured screenshot or live fetch.
Homepage
A strong hero, undercut by its own popup
The homepage converts its landings at 2.08% with a 57% bounce, by far the healthiest engagement on the site. It is also only 6% of landings: ads skip it and land cold on PDPs.
Live homepage, mobile · 15 Jul 2026
What we observed
  • Real lifestyle hero (a child holding the watch) with a benefit headline: "Det perfekte ur til dit barn". Genuinely good.
  • A Klaviyo popup ("Du har fået en Eksklusiv Rabat!") fires after ~5-7 seconds and covers the entire mobile fold, on every fresh page load, PDPs included.
  • The hero badge says "Fremragende" with green stars. The store's live Trustpilot score is 3.9, which Trustpilot labels "God". One click of fact-checking costs the badge its credibility.
  • Hero CTA is a vague "Se den her", and the rotating slideshow moves the message before it lands.
  • Navigation is flat: Alle produkter / Bestsellers / Tilbud / Pakker. No category entry points (børn, hjem, projektor) anywhere.
What to do
  • Delay the popup to exit intent or 30s+, and never over a PDP buy box.
  • Replace "Fremragende" with the true, checkable number: "4.000+ anmeldelser" or "+10.000 kunder". Honest numbers survive the click.
  • Make the CTA concrete: "Se KidsBuddy, fra 749 kr".
  • Add 3-4 category tiles above "Populært lige nu".
  • Keep the lifestyle hero. It is the best asset on the site.
The homepage is not the problem, it is the proof: when visitors land somewhere with story and context, bounce halves (57% vs 82% sitewide). The task is to bring that context to the PDPs, where 84% of landings actually arrive.
Collection / PLP
One flat list, no proof, no paths
/collections/produkter converts landings at 4.07%, second best on the site, but receives only 565 landing sessions in 90 days. The page works; nothing sends traffic to it.
Live collection, mobile · 15 Jul 2026
What we observed
  • 34 products in one flat "Alle produkter" grid: watches, projectors, blenders and KidsBuddy accessories side by side. Only price and stock filters, no categories.
  • No review stars on any product card, while the KidsBuddy accessory cards show green stars baked into the product photos themselves, which reads as fabricated proof.
  • Discount badges ("Spar 25%") on most cards + "Vejledende udsalgspris" anchors everywhere, a pattern shoppers now associate with dropship stores.
  • Clean 2-column mobile grid, fast to scan, prices visible. The basics are right.
What to do
  • Split into 3-4 real collections (Børn & tryghed, Sov & stemning, Hjem) and link them from nav + homepage tiles.
  • Show Loox stars + counts on cards for products that have reviews.
  • Add a "KidsBuddy tilbehør" collection and link it from the KidsBuddy PDP (owners come back for straps, chargers, glass).
  • Reserve strikethrough anchors for real campaign periods to protect price credibility.
Collection and Bestsellers landings convert at 4.07% and 4.76%, roughly double the PDP average, because shoppers who navigate there themselves arrive warm. More internal paths into these pages (category tiles, nav, PDP cross-links) is free conversion.
Product page · KidsBuddy
The money page sells hard and reassures little
24,331 landing sessions, 2.93% CVR, 56% of revenue. The buy box stacks offer on offer (25% off, ViaBill, SuperDeal, countdown), while the #1 customer doubt (GPS + app) is answered nowhere.
Live KidsBuddy PDP, mobile · 15 Jul 2026
What we observed
  • Good bones: 6 USP bullets, color variants, countdown to next-day dispatch, sticky ATC, ViaBill monthly price, 30-day guarantee below.
  • No review stars or count anywhere in the buy box. Loox sits far below the fold; the product twin kidsbuddy.dk shows "4.8/5 fra 2,200+ forældre" at the price.
  • The "SuperDeal" bundle is pre-selected: clicking the plain TILFØJ TIL KURV added 3 items (999 kr) when the shopper expected one for 749 kr.
  • Trust is outsourced to 11 affiliate "best in test" badges, while GPS accuracy and the SeTracker2 app (7 and 5 recent Trustpilot complaints) go unmentioned.
  • No video, no real app screenshots in context, no "how the GPS works" expectations block. Gallery is a stock render.
What to do
  • Pin Loox stars + review count directly under the title.
  • Flip the SuperDeal radio default ("1x Kidsbuddy 749 kr" pre-selected). Surprise items in the cart are refund complaints in waiting.
  • Add an honest GPS + app block: real SeTracker2 screens, "GPS er præcis udendørs, mindre præcis indendørs" expectations, setup help.
  • Add a 20-30s demo video (live tracking + a video call).
  • Add the anti-Xplora comparison: 749 kr once vs 1,599 kr + abonnement.
The math of honesty: this page needs 24,331 people a quarter to believe a 749 kr safety device for their child works. Every element that overstates (a "Fremragende" badge vs a 3.9 Trustpilot, an auto-added bundle, affiliate badges as proof) feeds the "dropshipping" suspicion that 4 recent reviews voice verbatim. The fixes above are additions of proof, not redesigns.
Product page · long tail
The same template cannot carry a 349 kr impulse buy
Moona Stjernehimmel: 3,548 landing sessions, 1.69% CVR, 87.6% bounce, 3m54s average visit. Glimmt løbecykel: 3,080 sessions at 0.91%. Beamly: 2,806 at 1.07%. Together: 9,434 sessions converting at half the store average.
Live Moona PDP, mobile · 15 Jul 2026
What we observed
  • Same template as KidsBuddy but with the trust stripped out: 3 thin USP bullets ("Magisk stemning i ethvert rum"), no testimonials, no badges, no guarantee block up top.
  • Gallery is a generic supplier render (product + remote on white), identical to what a shopper finds on any marketplace at half the price.
  • Same "Spar 22%" + "Vejledende udsalgspris" anchor pattern, which reads as markup, not value, without proof around it.
  • The Klaviyo discount popup interrupts these cold paid landings too.
  • No cross-sell into the rest of the catalog (a star projector buyer is a Noomu night-light buyer).
What to do
  • Build one stronger long-tail PDP template: lifestyle photo first (the room, dark, stars on), stars + count, delivery promise at ATC.
  • Shoot or source one photo in real context per hero product. One image is the whole gap.
  • Add "Købes ofte sammen" cross-sells between the kids products.
  • Roll the winner template to Glimmt and Beamly and measure against their 0.91% / 1.07% baselines.
  • Pause or fix Smart Cleaner (224 sessions, 0 orders, 2m26s of wasted ad clicks).
Visitors spend nearly 4 minutes on the Moona page and still do not buy: interest is there, belief is not. Lifting just Moona, Glimmt and Beamly to Kidsboard's ordinary 2.11% adds Est. +80 orders per quarter from traffic already being paid for.
Cart drawer
A good drawer with two silent leaks
GA4: 51.6% of add-to-carts reach checkout, and then only 45.3% of mobile checkouts finish. The drawer itself has the right furniture. Two details work against it.
Cart drawer after one ATC click · 15 Jul 2026
What we observed
  • Strong furniture: free-shipping progress ("Tillykke! Du har opnået gratis levering!"), a 1% charity selector, payment logos, "+10.000 GLADE KUNDER" bar.
  • The 749 kr decision became a 999 kr total: the pre-selected SuperDeal added the guarantee (299 kr, discounted to 249 kr in the bundle) and cable without an explicit choice. Sticker shock at the drawer is the classic cart→checkout killer.
  • A manual "Jeg accepterer handelsbetingelserne" checkbox gates the checkout button. Unticked, the CTA silently fails: pure friction, and not required at this step for Danish shops (Shopify checkout collects acceptance).
  • The trust bar claims 5 stars; Trustpilot says 3.9. Same credibility tax as the hero.
What to do
  • Make the bundle opt-in on the PDP so the drawer total matches the expectation set at the ATC click.
  • Remove the terms checkbox from the drawer (keep acceptance at checkout).
  • Swap the 5-star claim for a real, checkable number.
  • Add one-tap express pay (MobilePay / Shop Pay) in the drawer for the 89% on mobile.
  • Keep the charity selector and the free-shipping bar. Both are genuinely good.
Test order matters here: the checkbox removal and express-pay placement target the 45.3% mobile checkout completion directly and are cheap to run. The bundle opt-in test protects both the cart→checkout step and the pipeline of refund complaints that feeds Trustpilot.

A/B test plans
The three highest-priority tests, written to the pre-test brief standard: observation, hypothesis, variant spec, metrics and guardrails. Baselines are frozen from this audit's 90-day GA4 pull.

Observation

GA4 funnel: only 7.2% of product viewers add to cart. The KidsBuddy buy box shows no review stars or count, while the product twin kidsbuddy.dk shows "4.8/5 fra 2,200+ forældre" at the same 749 kr. The #1 objection in recent Trustpilot reviews (GPS accuracy, 7 mentions; SeTracker2 app, 5 mentions) is answered nowhere on the page.

Hypothesis

If we pin the Loox rating + review count under the title and add an honest GPS + app expectations block with real app screens, then add-to-cart rate will rise, because the two doubts that stop a 749 kr parent purchase get answered at the decision point.

What we are testing

  • Loox stars + count pinned under the product title
  • "Sådan virker GPS'en" block: real SeTracker2 screens + honest accuracy expectations
  • e-mærket buyer protection + "749 kr once, ingen abonnement" line near the ATC

Observation

The "SuperDeal" bundle is pre-selected: clicking the plain TILFØJ TIL KURV added 3 items totalling 999 kr when the shopper expected one item at 749 kr. GA4: only 51.6% of add-to-carts reach checkout. Trustpilot 1-star reviews cluster on refund friction, the downstream cost of surprise totals.

Hypothesis

If the bundle becomes an explicit opt-in (single item pre-selected, SuperDeal as a visible choice), then cart to checkout rate will rise, because the drawer total matches the price the shopper committed to at the ATC click.

What we are testing

  • Flip the existing radio default: "1x Kidsbuddy · 749 kr" pre-selected, SuperDeal as the upgrade
  • Bundle contents itemized before the click, not after
  • Guarantee (299 kr) also available as a single checkbox add-on
