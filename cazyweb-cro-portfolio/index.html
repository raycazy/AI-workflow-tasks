PH Import All PDPs A/B/C May 2026 All devices

Trust at the buy moment lifted revenue per visitor +4.1%.

01
Control
Scroll to the buy box
VariantWinner
Scroll to the buy box
1

What changed: a strip of assurance icons (free shipping over 300 kr, 100,000+ customers, 1 to 3 day delivery) was added directly below the bundle selector, at the moment of decision.

Observation

The recommended product above add-to-cart earned about 1% click-through on desktop and 0.7% on mobile, against 7% for the ATC button. The upsell pulled 0.1% of clicks. The space at the buy moment was not helping people buy.

Hypothesis

Because reassurance at the point of decision reinforces both trust and the bundle choice, moving assurance icons below the bundles will lift add-to-cart rate, conversion and revenue per visitor.

PH Import PDP A/B June 2026 Mobile

A compact bundle carousel raised mobile conversion +16.6%.

02
Control
Stacked quantity grid
VariantWinner
One-row carousel, ATC in view
1

What changed: the tall stacked quantity grid became a single-row carousel with the highest-value bundles (12, 6, 3) shown first, so the add-to-cart button stays in view.

Observation

On mobile the stacked quantity grid filled the first fold and pushed add-to-cart far down the page. Buyers defaulted to the smallest pack because it sat first in the list.

Hypothesis

Because a compact, highest-first selector anchors buyers on larger bundles and brings ATC into view sooner, reordering and collapsing the tiers will lift order value and revenue per visitor on mobile.

Research to result · featured case study

On-site search: one usability fix, two significant wins.

A worked example of the full method for My 1st Years, from the data that started it to the significance that ended it.

Making search visible turned browsers into buyers.

The signal

Only 8.5% of users touched on-site search, yet those who did converted at 12.5%, against 5.6% for sessions without search. Search buyers converted at roughly 2.25 times the rate. The lever was hiding in plain sight behind a small magnifier icon.

The change

We replaced the search icon with a visible search bar on desktop and mobile, and added microcopy inside it to draw attention. Nothing else on the page moved.

The call

The variant won on both goals at significance and was recommended for rollout, with the search-and-purchase journey queued as the next optimization.
