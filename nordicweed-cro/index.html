Schedule A · Stage Multipliers
Tests Further from Purchase Carry More Projection Risk
The stage multiplier discounts the performance fee for upper-funnel tests where revenue impact is estimated rather than directly measured. Approved in the pre-test brief, before the test runs.
100%
RPV · AOV · Purchase CVR · Checkout CVR · Post-purchase
Revenue measured directly by the testing software. No projection needed.
75%
Cart to checkout · PDP add to cart
One step from purchase. Light projection of downstream behavior.
50%
Collection to PDP · PDP engagement
Two steps from purchase. Multiple downstream conversion rates to project.
25%
Homepage to collection · Navigation · Site search
Three or more steps from purchase. Heaviest projection assumptions.
