Executive Summary
Six Findings That Move Revenue
The store is healthy and box led. 11.88M SEK and 11,689 orders in 90 days at a 2.6% session CVR. Wine boxes convert far above single bottles (intro box 5.54% vs bottles near 2.2%).
Wine boxes are the growth engine. Merchandise them harder.
Delivery is the number one trust wound. The site promises "1-3 dagar" but 31% of low star reviews report 7 to 16 days. 86% of complaints are about delivery, and loyal repeat buyers are churning on it.
Fix the promise, not the wine. This is a retention leak, not just acquisition.
The legality question is unanswered. In a monopoly market, a first time Swedish buyer needs to know this is legal. dittvin explains nothing, while KRAN and Winefamly reassure directly. Fear tips to "Tror detta r bedrägeri".
80.8% of users are new. The trust block is missing exactly where it converts.
The cart to checkout leak is the biggest recoverable pool. 60.2% of add to carts never begin checkout (32,859 users in 90 days). Shipping surprise and no clear next step.
Est. 1.5 to 1.8M SEK per quarter recoverable at current AOV.
Revenue rides on one channel. Facebook paid drives 41% of sessions and 43.6% of all orders. A single algorithm change or ad ban would hit hard.
Grow email (already 16% of orders) and organic (a weak 2.03% CVR) to de risk.
Tracking is noisy and partly misconfigured. Four GA4 properties fire at once, a Google Ads conversion tag fires on the homepage, and 29% of sessions land as "(not set)". Real CVR on clean traffic is closer to 3.6%.
Clean data first, then optimize. The store is better than the dashboard shows.

03
The Trust Equation
In Swedish online alcohol, two levers decide the first purchase: is it legal, and will it arrive. dittvin is silent on the first and unreliable on the second.
Market Context · Highest Leverage Gap
Nobody Told the Swede It Is Legal
Sweden runs the Systembolaget monopoly. dittvin lawfully ships as a Danish cross border private importer (Vinhuset ApS). It never explains this, so doubt fills the gap.
What dittvin does today

Silence on legality

Zero mention of Systembolaget, private import, Swedish alcohol tax, or that VAT is included. The Danish operator identity is buried in the footer. The genuine assets (a 4.7 Trustpilot, award medals, Løvens Hule TV backing) sit far from the point of doubt.

"Så in i norden dåligt. leveransen har inte kommit fast det har gått 2 veckor. Tror detta r bedrägeri. VARNING" (I think this is fraud. WARNING)
What the best rival does

KRAN states it plainly

"Vi lyder under den svenska alkohollagstiftningen och regleras av svenska Skatteverket som distansförsäljare." Winefamly reassures with "Alla priser inkl. svensk alkoholskatt & moms." Winefinder, Winefamly and Supervin also show the Trygg E-handel trust mark. dittvin shows none of it.

The fix: a plain "Is this legal? Yes, and here is how" block on the PDP and cart, with the age check, the Danish import model, and tax included stated up front.
Voice of Customer · Strategy
Three Beliefs to Destroy
Every first time Swedish buyer carries these. Each has a specific, evidenced counter already available to dittvin.
1

"Buying wine privately in Sweden might be illegal or shady."

Destroy it with a plain language legality block: lawful Danish private import, age verified, Vinhuset ApS named, tax and VAT included, and the 4.7 on 5,985 Trustpilot proof placed at the point of hesitation.

80.8% of users are new buyers
2

"They will not deliver on time."

This is the true belief driving the negative tail. Destroy it by fixing the promise, not the wine. Replace "1-3 dagar" with an honest order specific ETA and proactive status updates, and let customers pick a delivery window.

86% of complaints are delivery
3

"The 'quality wines' are just cheap sweet plonk."

Destroy it with real per wine specs on box PDPs: grape, region, a dry to sweet scale, and the Vivino score you already hold. A box should not be a blind gamble.

14% of complaints cite taste

Heuristic · Entry
Two Walls Before a Single Product
Cold Facebook traffic (41% of sessions) lands and hits a legally required age gate stacked on top of a spin the wheel email popup, both before any wine is seen.
Live mobile entry · captured 2026-07-23
Double interstitial. The age check ("bekräfta att du är 20 år eller äldre") is legally needed, but it stacks on a "Snurra på hjulet" wheel popup. Two dismissals before the first product.
Merge into one gate. Verify a cookie remembers the choice so returning users are not re gated.
Turn the age gate into a trust moment. Right now it only blocks. Add one line: lawful Danish import, tax included. It is the perfect place to answer the legality question.
Emoji heavy promo bar. The top bar (party and clock emoji) clashes with a "kvalitetsviner" premium promise on a wine brand.
Chat and Klaviyo present. Support and capture tools are live. The issue is stacking and sequence, not absence.
Heuristic · Homepage
Strong Bones, Two Real Cracks
Clear category nav and box carousels drive a 24.9% bounce. But blanket discounting and broken sections undercut the premium story.
Homepage · mobile capture
Clear category circles. Rött, Vitt, Mousserande, Rosé, Gin, Vinlådor, Erbjudanden, Abonnemang, Alkoholfri 0%. Easy orientation, with Vivino badges on products.
Everything is on sale. Every product wears a red discount flag (-45%, -51%) plus "Spara X kr". Permanent discounting erodes urgency and reads as generic, not premium.
Reserve discounting for real offers. Merchandise awards and Vivino scores as the proof instead.
Sections render inconsistently. "Upplev både nya..." and "Kunskap och inspiration" loaded as empty bands on one capture and filled on the next, consistent with the 90+ JS console errors.
Flaky merchandising blocks. Fix the errors and confirm sections render reliably on mobile Safari.
Trust assets buried. The Danish operator line and Løvens Hule backing sit only in the footer, far from where doubt occurs.
Heuristic · Collection and PDP
Good Buy Box, Missing Reassurance
The PDP has a sticky add to cart, social proof and a free shipping bar, but no legality module, thin box detail, and Danish text leaking through.
Fantini box PDP · mobile
Solid fundamentals. Sticky add to cart, strikethrough price, "+75.000 vinälskare" social proof, Trustpilot and award badges, and a free shipping progress bar.
No legality block at the decision point. The exact place KRAN and Winefamly reassure, dittvin stays silent. This suppresses the 64% who view but never add.
Thin box detail. Only 3 gallery images for a 12 bottle box, and no per wine grape, sweetness or Vivino breakdown. Buyers are asked to gamble.
Customers explicitly ask: "Jag saknar en beskrivning av respektive vin."
Danish leaks and shipping caveats. Page title reads "Kjøb hos Dittvin.se" (Danish), and free shipping is quietly limited to the Airmee carrier, which confuses buyers who then see a Bring fee.
