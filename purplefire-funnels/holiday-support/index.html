On the 15 minute call

1. Ask

  1. What is your Black Friday offer, and does it change for Cyber Monday?
  2. What is live already, and what is not built yet?
  3. Who builds your pages today?
  4. When is your code freeze?
  5. What happens on the site after Cyber Monday, through December and into January?

2. Propose the funnel

"I'll set up your holiday funnel with you, in four parts, and get each part live as soon as it's ready."

  • The offer: we shape it together, from what has won in 5,000+ A/B tests.
  • Landing pages: the sale page, the Cyber Monday pages, gift guides.
  • Cart and checkout: cart offers, and checkout offers where your Shopify plan allows them.
  • After the order: a post purchase offer, and your January sale pages built in December.
  • In January: every page built now can be A/B tested.
  • Scope: unlimited landing pages, design and development, until Thu 31 Dec 2026.

3. Holiday offer pointers

  • Tiered offers: a bigger reward at two or three spend levels.
  • Gift with purchase: for brands whose margin cannot take a deeper discount.
  • Bundles: ready made sets at one price, which double as gifts.
  • Deadlines and shipping cutoffs: one clear end time per offer, and the last order day for Christmas delivery.
  • The Cyber Monday swap: pages and banners ready before Black Friday, so Mon 30 Nov is a switch.
  • Gift guides: by who the gift is for, or by price.
  • January sale: planned now, not built in a rush.

4. What they give you on day one

  • A staff or collaborator account on the store
  • Logo, fonts, product photos and campaign images
  • The offer and its dates
  • One person who approves each page

Keep it honest

What goes live before Fri 27 Nov depends on when they start: a yes by Wed 18 Nov leaves room for a few pieces Est., so agree which come first. A full redesign or new theme is not part of this, and no single client's result is quoted on the call.

US brands are mostly off Thu 26 Nov, so aim for everything live by Wed 25 Nov. Once you are full, offer the next brand a 15 minute call about January A/B tests.
