The other half of the result

Two tests lost. Both were cheap, and both taught us the same thing.

These are the results that make the programme worth running. Each of these was a change SportGym might reasonably have shipped permanently. Two short test windows caught both.

T08 · Sticky announcement bar with three value props
Taking the viewport costs more than the message adds
50,46%drop in conversion rate · true effect between 11% and 73%
Mobile was hit hardest: conversion 4,80% to 2,12%, cart abandonment 38,24% to 55,00%. Product page visits were unchanged, so the damage began after visitors reached the product. A permanent header took the screen the decision needed.
T05 · Cart drawer redesign
Never ask a customer to make the same decision twice
51%drop in tracked add to cart · 37 to 14 users
The recommendation carousel offered a 20 kg and a 24 kg kettlebell to a shopper who had just chosen 16 kg. Those are substitutes, not accessories. The drawer reopened a decision the customer had already closed.
Both tests ran on short windows and small budgets. Two weeks of testing prevented two permanent site changes, each of which would have cost real revenue every month for as long as it stayed live.
What the quarter proved

Every result this quarter says the same thing about this buyer.

Both winners put a deciding fact where the decision happens. Specs under the price. Attributes on the card. Both losers took something away from that moment: the sticky bar took the viewport, the cart drawer took back the decision. SportGym sells considered purchases from 2.495 kr to 69.900 kr. This buyer is comparing, and they convert when the comparison is easy and the decision stays made.

Why Q4 is different

Q4 is the quarter where one point of conversion is worth the most.

What changes
  • Black Friday falls on 27 November, Cyber Monday on 30 November. That week concentrates the year's highest intent traffic into five days.
  • Christmas turns the biggest category into a gift category. Trampolines and PlayBase already carry around 52% of product sales, and both are bought for someone else in December.
  • Baskets get bigger. The catalogue reaches 69.900 kr, and 9 of the 13 treadmills sit above 5.000 kr.
What is still unfinished
  • The filters do not match the attributes that sell. 131 trampolines filter on størrelse, højde and form. 13 treadmills filter on nothing but stock and price. Verified live, 24 August.
  • No monthly payment option appears anywhere on a product page. We checked the TR600i page for ViaBill, Klarna, Anyday and delbetaling and found none of them.
  • Stock and delivery timing never reach the product card, even though the product page already promises delivery in 1 to 2 working days and pickup in Aarhus within the hour.
The constraint that shapes the plan: nothing new should ship between 20 November and 1 December. Every change Q4 needs must be built, tested and read before that window opens, which makes October the decisive month and September the build month.

Filter on the attributes that already sell

SPG | A/B | PLP | Filtre på trampoliner og løbebånd
Observation
  • T03 proved which attributes decide the purchase, and those attributes now sit on every card. Not one of them can be filtered on.
  • Løbebånd: 13 models from 2.495 kr to 69.900 kr, filtered only by Tilgængelighed and Pris. Motor, maks. brugervægt, tophastighed and sammenklappelig are printed on every card and filterable on none of them.
  • Trampoliner: 131 products, filtered by størrelse, højde and form. All three are dimensions. Montering, maks. testvægt and bedst til, which the cards themselves treat as decisive, are missing.
Hypothesis

IF shoppers can filter by the same attributes the cards already use to sell, WE EXPECT a higher collection to product click through rate and a higher collection conversion rate, BECAUSE T03 already proved that surfacing a decision attribute earlier lifts collection conversion by 17,15%, and a filter is that same information turned into a shortcut.

What we are testing
  1. Løbebånd: add motor, maks. brugervægt, tophastighed and sammenklappelig to the existing two filters.
  2. Trampoliner: add montering (fritstående, delvist nedgravet, nedgravet), maks. testvægt and bedst til alongside the existing three.
  3. Applied filters shown as removable chips with live result counts.

Q02 PDP · Collection Pillar A · Finish the pattern Impact Medium Ease High · fastest win

The "Bedst til" line

SPG | A/B | PDP + PLP | Who this model is actually for
Observation
  • Spec highlights won by 25,93%, and every attribute it shows is a number: motor, tophastighed, maks. brugervægt, design.
  • Numbers tell a shopper what a machine does. They do not tell them whether it is the right one for them.
  • One category already does this. Trampoline cards carry "BEDST TIL: Hyppig brug & højere hop", and it is the only attribute on the site written in the customer's own language.
Hypothesis

IF we add one plain language "Bedst til" line to every product card and to the PDP spec row, WE EXPECT a higher add to cart rate and a higher collection to product click through rate, BECAUSE both winning tests worked by removing a step of comparison, and the last step a shopper still has to take alone is translating specifications into a decision about themselves.

What we are testing
  1. "Bedst til" added as a further attribute, written per model rather than per category.
  2. On the product page it sits directly beneath the existing spec row.
  3. Variant B pairs it with an honest "Ikke til" line, which is the strongest form of the same signal.

Stock and delivery on the product card

SPG | A/B | PLP | På lager og leveringstid, før klikket
Observation
  • The product page already promises "På lager, 1-2 hverdages levering" and "Klar til afhentning i dag, typisk inden for 1 time" in Risskov. Both verified live on 24 August.
  • None of that reaches the collection card. A shopper comparing 131 trampolines cannot see which of them will arrive in time.
  • In December, arrival date stops being a detail and becomes a purchase criterion.
Hypothesis

IF stock status and delivery timing appear on the product card, WE EXPECT more qualified product page visits and a higher collection conversion rate, BECAUSE a shopper buying a gift filters on arrival date before they filter on features, and SportGym's own warehouse is a genuine advantage that the card currently hides.

What we are testing
  1. "På lager · levering 1-2 hverdage" on every in stock card.
  2. "Kan afhentes i Aarhus i dag" where local pickup applies.
  3. From late November, an explicit "Fremme inden jul" marker driven by the carrier cutoff date.

"Find dit løbebånd" guided selling

SPG | A/B | PLP + Home | Four questions, a shortlist, a reason for each
Observation
  • Thirteen treadmills from 2.495 kr to 69.900 kr, a 28 times price spread, and the only way to choose is to open product pages one at a time and hold them in your head.
  • The collection page already promises the help: "Hos SportGym hjælper vi dig med at finde det løbebånd, der passer præcist til dine behov." Nothing on the page delivers it.
  • The same problem sits across 131 trampolines, at higher volume.
Hypothesis

IF a four question quiz returns a shortlist of two or three models with one reason for each, WE EXPECT a higher collection to product click through rate and a higher conversion rate on those sessions, BECAUSE the promise of expert guidance is already made in the copy, and the quiz is the first place the site actually keeps it.

What we are testing
  1. Four questions: use case (gang, motion, løb, pro), budget, maks. brugervægt, plads og foldbarhed.
  2. A ranked shortlist of two to three models, each with a single sentence explaining the match.
  3. Entry points at the top of the collection and from the homepage kondition tile.

Q05 Product pages Pillar B · The big decision Impact Medium Ease Medium

Comparison table and PDP FAQ

SPG | A/B | PDP | This model, the tier above, the tier below
Observation
  • The TR900i costs 12.900 kr and the TR600i costs 13.900 kr. The cheaper one has the larger motor. Nothing on either page explains why, so the shopper is left to work it out or leave.
  • The collection page carries a FAQ. The product page does not, so warranty, assembly, bulky item returns and delivery go unanswered at the moment of decision.
  • The comparison is happening anyway, across browser tabs. The site that hosts it keeps the session.
Hypothesis

IF each product page carries a three column comparison against the tier above and below, plus a five entry FAQ, WE EXPECT a higher add to cart rate on models above 8.000 kr, BECAUSE the two questions blocking a considered purchase are "is this the right one" and "what happens if it is not", and neither is currently answered on the page where the decision is made.

What we are testing
  1. A three column, eight row table: this model, tier up, tier down, with differences highlighted.
  2. A five entry FAQ under the description: garanti, montering, retur af store varer, delbetaling, levering.
  3. Priority rollout to every model above 8.000 kr.

Monthly payment as a co primary price

SPG | A/B | PDP + PLP | Fra X kr/md, beside the total
Observation
  • There is no financing option anywhere on the product page. We checked the TR600i page on 24 August for ViaBill, Klarna, Anyday and delbetaling. None of them appear.
  • Pay later is a default expectation in Denmark above roughly 2.000 kr, and 9 of the 13 treadmills sit above 5.000 kr.
  • A 69.900 kr sticker price is a wall. The same product expressed as a monthly figure is a decision.
Hypothesis

IF a monthly figure sits beside the total price on every product above 1.500 kr and on the matching collection cards, WE EXPECT a higher add to cart rate on the higher priced half of the catalogue, BECAUSE monthly framing lowers perceived cost at exactly the point in the year when SportGym's baskets are largest.

What we are testing
  1. First confirm the account status. T15 is marked deployed on the board but nothing is live on the page.
  2. Price block: total price, with the monthly figure directly beneath it as a co primary anchor.
  3. Collection cards show "fra X kr/md" on models above 5.000 kr.

Black Friday price confidence

SPG | A/B | Sitewide | Prisgaranti instead of countdown
Observation
  • SportGym already runs Prisgaranti, "Vi matcher prisen", in the announcement bar and in the buy box trust row. Verified live, 24 August.
  • Most of the catalogue already shows a "Vejl. pris" strike through, and several cards carry an explicit "SPAR 3.000 kr" badge. In Black Friday week every competitor will look the same.
  • T08 proved that adding permanent promotional furniture to this site halves conversion. Black Friday week is the most expensive possible week to learn that a second time.
Hypothesis

IF Black Friday leads with price guarantee and a stated saving rather than countdown urgency, WE EXPECT conversion to hold or rise through the week, BECAUSE the one promotional element this site has already tested badly was the one that occupied the screen, and a price match promise is the single claim a competitor's discount cannot undercut.

What we are testing
  1. A consistent saving amount badge on card and product page, using the numbers already in the catalogue.
  2. Prisgaranti raised into the price block rather than sitting in the trust row.
  3. No sticky bar, no countdown, no new viewport furniture. That constraint is part of the test.

Q08 Sitewide · Category Pillar C · The Q4 calendar Impact High Ease Medium

Christmas gift mode

SPG | A/B | Sitewide | Fremme inden jul, og byt indtil marts
Observation
  • Trampolines and PlayBase carry around 52% of product sales, and in December both are bought for someone else.
  • A gift buyer has three questions a self buyer does not: will it arrive in time, can the recipient return it, and is it the right size for them. None of the three is currently answered in gift terms.
  • 365 dages returret answers the second question better than almost any competitor, and it currently sits as a small item in a trust row.
Hypothesis

IF the site publishes a dated Christmas delivery promise, opens a gift oriented route into the biggest categories, and reframes the 365 day return for gift giving, WE EXPECT a higher conversion rate on December traffic, BECAUSE the objections a gift buyer carries are different from the ones the site was built to answer, and the strongest answer SportGym already owns is currently the quietest thing on the page.

What we are testing
  1. A dated promise: "Bestil inden [dato], og den er fremme inden jul", taken from the carrier cutoff and shown sitewide.
  2. A gift route: "Gaver til hele familien", with size and age guidance across trampoliner and aktiv leg.
  3. 365 dages returret restated in gift language, for example "Byt indtil marts".

Q09 Product pages Pillar C · The Q4 calendar Impact Medium Ease Medium

"Komplet dit setup" accessory cross sell

SPG | A/B | PDP | Complete the decision, never reopen it
Observation
  • The TR600i page already offers one accessory, an underlagsmåtte at 595 kr, under "Købes ofte sammen med". It is the right idea, stopped at a single item.
  • T05 failed for a specific reason: the cart recommended a 20 kg and 24 kg kettlebell to a customer who had just chosen 16 kg. Accessories complete a decision. Alternatives reopen it.
  • Q4 baskets are the largest of the year, and every accessory attached at the product page is margin that costs no new traffic.
Hypothesis

IF the product page offers a curated set of genuinely complementary accessories and never an alternative model, WE EXPECT a higher average order value and more items per order with no reduction in add to cart, BECAUSE the cart test showed the damage comes from substitution rather than from recommendation itself.

What we are testing
  1. Rebuild the module as "Komplet dit setup" with three to five curated accessories per category.
  2. A strict rule enforced in the code: nothing from the same category as the product being viewed.
  3. Add to cart directly from the module, with no navigation away from the page.
