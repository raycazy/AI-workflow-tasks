Questions about UCollect.

Who owns HGA?

UCollect. UCollect acquired HGA in 2025, and HGA now runs as the grading service on the UCollect platform.

If UCollect runs a marketplace, can that affect my grade?

No. HGA grades independently to a published standard, and every card is graded blind: the grader never knows whose card it is. HGA does not buy, sell or own cards.

Is this the same HGA as before?

Same name, rebuilt under new ownership: a new grading standard published in full, new HGA Glass and HGA Poly housings, and a chip in every label that ties the slab to its record.

What can I do on UCollect?

Buy, sell and trade collectibles, get cards graded by HGA, keep them in a vault, and have shipping and insurance handled for you. UCollect is opening invite only, starting with buying, selling and grading.
