The presale

Six things to know.

01

What you’re buying

A prepaid grading pass for one card, in HGA Glass or HGA Poly. Every subgrade, the Grade Report, the chip and the case come with the pass.

02

What it costs

HGA Glass $200 a card now, $600 at launch (save $400). HGA Poly $29 a card now, $150 at launch. The presale price ends Jan 31. From Feb 1 prices return to launch pricing, or earlier while supplies last.

03

You pick the day

Buy from Nov 23. From Jan 18, book your grading date on the HGA Grading Calendar and send your cards any time before your date. Grading starts Feb 1.

04

Extra fees

None on cards worth $10,000 or less. Above that, 2.5% of the estimated raw market value over $10,000, charged only on the amount above $10,000.

05

What you can send

We grade all sports and all TCG, in every card size. Standard cards, oversized cards and booklets are all in the presale, in Glass or Poly. Oversized cards are $600 in Glass and $90 in Poly. Booklets are $300 in Glass and $45 in Poly for one fold, plus $100 (Glass) or $15 (Poly) for each extra fold.

06

Your pass

One pass per card, on your account. Buy up to 100 passes per housing in each order, as many orders as you like. All sales are final, and passes can’t be transferred.

How HGA works.

See your card, the process and the proof.

01 / RECEIVE ITChecked in and documented

Once your card arrives at HGA it's checked in and documented, ready for your grading date.

02 / INSPECT ITInspected, scanned, identified

Your card is carefully inspected, scanned and identified before grading, so its condition and identity are documented from the start.

03 / GRADE ITSoftware assisted, human graded

High resolution images and software assist on centering. A human grader evaluates corners, edges and surface, with included subgrades and a full grading report explaining your final grade.

04 / PROTECT ITSealed and chipped

Your graded card is secured inside your selected Poly or premium Glass and Metal housing, complete with an RFID chip and digital verification.

05 / REVEAL ITLive, from delivery to grade

See your final grade, subgrades and grading report in your HGA account.
