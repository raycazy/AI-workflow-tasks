04
Why It Kept Failing

The blocker is Shopflo, not Shopify

Shopflo handles 98% of your orders. It replaces the Shopify checkout entirely.

Almost every bundle app calculates its price inside its own cart drawer or a draft order. Shopflo never sees that layer, so the bundle price is dropped or the box splits into full price items at checkout. That is why app after app looked broken. The apps were fine. The checkout could not read them.

Two facts change everything. Your 4 flavors are already separate products with their own inventory. And Shopflo does honor Shopify backend automatic discounts. That is the seam we build through.

What survives Shopflo vs what breaks
  • Survives. Native Shopify line items. Each sachet added as a real cart line.
  • Survives. Backend automatic discounts keyed on total quantity. The tier pricing.
  • Survives. First party Shopify Bundles, officially supported by Shopflo since Sept 2025.
  • Breaks. Apps that price inside their own cart drawer or draft order.
  • Breaks. Discount code based bundles and most cart transform price overrides.

How We Build It

One path we recommend, two safe fallbacks

ApproachHow it worksTrade-off
Theme native builder A "pick up to 8" picker built into the theme. Each sachet is added as its own Shopify line item. A backend automatic discount keyed on total quantity delivers the tier price. Per flavor inventory decrements natively. No Shopify Plus needed. Cart shows separate sachet lines, not one merged bundle line. Arguably clearer for the buyer. Recommended
Shopify Bundles packs Model 4 pack, 6 pack and 8 pack as first party Shopify Bundles products, each priced at its tier, each letting the buyer choose a flavor per slot. Discrete pack sizes, not a continuous slider. The only bundle Shopflo officially supports. Fallback A
Simple Bundles app A continuous build your own box app with component level inventory and native line items. Commit only after Shopflo confirms in writing it renders the expanded components and price. Fallback B

The point: we already have a working architecture that fits your exact stack. This is a build, not an experiment.

A live store is never finished. The retainer is an always on testing and fixing engine, not a report you file away.
