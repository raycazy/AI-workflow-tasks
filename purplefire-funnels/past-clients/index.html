You write to each of them: their own winner is still live on their store, here is the result you recorded, and would 15 minutes in January help to plan Q1.

The idea

Every store in the list below still runs at least one winner from an A/B test PurpleFire ran for it. You send your contact there a short message that shows them their own winner, still live, with the result as recorded in your log. The ask is a 15 minute call in January about what to test next.

Hypothesis

A founder may not book a call now because of Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the holidays, so the message asks for a 15 minute call in January, and a one word reply ("January") is enough. It is wrong if no client on the list replies with a yes, "January" or a question within ten working days of Day 3.

Cost

No media spend. Every message goes from your own inbox.

Message

Three short messages to each client on the list: Day 1, Day 2 and Day 3. Fill the slots from that client's card in Assets.

Who, to whom and when

Who sends
You, from your own inbox.
To whom
Your contact at each client in the list on Strategy 1. You already have these contacts.
When
Day 1 is any working day outside Mon 23 Nov to Tue 1 Dec 2026, picked so Day 2 and Day 3 land outside it too: by Wed 18 Nov, or from Wed 2 Dec. Day 2 and Day 3 go on the next two working days, only to people who have not replied.

Nothing goes to a Danish contact until a legal read confirms it is allowed.

The three messages

These are people who know you, so three days in a row is fine. Each message is shorter than the one before.

How to use: on the day you send Day 1, open the page from the card and take the screenshot named there, so "is still on your {page} today" is true when they read it. Day 2 and Day 3 go as replies in the same thread.

Fill from the card: paste the card's Day 1 lines over the two sentences "{winner} is still on your {page} today. In our A/B test it won: {result}, as recorded in our log." That one paste fills {winner}, {page} and {result}. If a card shows more than one winner, Day 1 names the first.

Before Day 2: read the next test idea on the card and check it on the page yourself.

After Day 3: nobody writes again unless they reply.

When they reply

Answer the same working day. A yes gets three 15 minute times in January.
