Brand essence

Borders, made effortless.

A visa is a high-stakes, high-anxiety purchase. A rejection can cancel a honeymoon or sink a business deal. Blue Atlas removes that fear: an expert partner that carries the burden of paperwork, deadlines, and embassy rules so the traveller can simply look forward to the trip. "Atlas" is the world and the figure who shoulders its weight. "Blue" is the calm of clear skies.

It is

  • Calm, reassuring, and in control
  • Light, photo-forward, and premium
  • Transparent about fees, timelines, and odds
  • Human experts, backed by clear technology
  • Visa and travel insurance as one promise

It is not

  • Cluttered, loud, or discount-driven
  • Cold government-portal bureaucracy
  • A generic blue corporate travel agency
  • Hype, emoji, or exclamation marks
  • Vague about what a service actually costs

Logo

Logo usage

A meridian globe with a single gold pin (your destination, marked) paired with the Hanken Grotesk wordmark in semibold. Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the globe. On photography, place it directly with no box, on a darkened area for legibility.

Do
  • Keep the gold pin gold on every surface
  • Use the mark alone for app icons and avatars
  • Darken busy photos behind the reversed lockup
Do not
  • Recolour the wordmark gold or azure
  • Stretch, rotate, or add a drop shadow
  • Place the dark lockup on a dark photo

Form elements

Forms are the heart of a visa product. Inputs are large and forgiving with a 14px radius and an azure focus ring. Every field that can be explained, is explained, because uncertainty is what makes applicants abandon.

Components

Lead capture form

The site's primary goal is to capture a qualified enquiry, so the form is the main conversion, not a generic "book now". It runs in two modes. Generic (Contact page, homepage): the visitor picks what they need. Page-level (a specific visa or destination page): the service and destination are captured automatically from the page, so the visitor does not re-pick.

Mode A · Generic contact form (with service selector)

Tell us where you are headed.

Share a few details and a visa expert calls back, usually within one business day, with your documents, timeline, and a quote.

  • A real human reply within 1 business day
  • No payment to enquire, no obligation
  • Documents, timeline, and odds up front

By submitting you agree to our privacy policy. We never share your details.

Mode B · Page-level form (service & destination auto-captured)

One less thing to pick.

On a destination or service page, the visitor already told us what they want by being there. We pre-fill it and hide the selector, so the form is shorter and converts better.

Auto-captured at build time (hidden fields)
source_service = "Tourist visa"
source_page = "/visas/japan-tourist"

The backend fills these from the page the lead came from, so every enquiry is routed and attributed without the visitor doing extra work.

System

Voice & copy

Calm, expert, and plain-spoken. We reduce anxiety with clarity, not exclamation marks. We say what something costs and how long it takes.

"We carry the paperwork. You see the world."

We sound like
  • "Your documents are with the embassy. We expect a decision by Friday."
  • "One photo was the wrong size. We fixed it before submitting."
  • "Tourist visa, all fees included, from $49."
  • "A dedicated expert, from apply to approved."
We never sound like
  • "Apply now!!! Cheapest visas guaranteed!"
  • "Why choose us? Because we are awesome!"
  • "Visa processing fees may apply (see terms)."
  • Dense legal jargon or embassy acronyms unexplained
