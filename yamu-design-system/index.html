Brand essence

Clear, credible, conversion led.

Yamu is a CRO and design studio. The brand should read like a sharp analyst who also has taste: data first, never loud, always actionable.

It is

  • Confident and evidence based
  • Calm, modern, generous with space
  • Orange used as a precise highlight, not a wash
  • Numbers and outcomes front and center
  • Plain English, short sentences

It is not

  • Hype, salesy, or paratactic ("Fast. Simple. Done.")
  • Cluttered or gradient heavy
  • Orange on orange on orange
  • Jargon or vague growth speak
  • Em dashes, ever

Logo

The mark and the wordmark.

A two tone orange "V" formed from a person and a checkmark, paired with the YAMU wordmark. Give it room and keep it orange.

Primary lockup on navy (left) and on white (right). The orange mark holds on both. Minimum clearspace equals the height of the "V" mark on all sides.
Do not recolor the mark outside the orange family.
Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the lockup.
Do not place on a busy photo without a scrim.
Do not add shadows, outlines, or effects.

Voice & tone

Write like a sharp analyst.

Plain English, short sentences, every claim backed by a number. No hype, no em dashes, no anticipating objections.

Do

"Mobile is 71% of sessions but converts at half the desktop rate. That gap is the opportunity."

"Three tests for Q3, ranked by expected lift."

Lead with the metric. Name the source. Recommend one clear action.

Avoid

"Unlock explosive growth and skyrocket your revenue!"

"Fast. Simple. Done."

No paratactic fragments, no growth speak, no em dashes. Use periods, colons, and parentheses instead.
