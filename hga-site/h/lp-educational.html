The REAL Reasons Why You Want Your Cards Graded

How much do you REALLY know about your card collection? From counterfeit collectibles and altered surfaces to misleading condition claims, not every card is exactly what it appears to be.

Counterfeit Concerns Almost Every Collector Faces

TCG Collectors: Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic, etc.

  • Counterfeit Cards & Disguised Proxies

    Some counterfeit or proxy cards are presented as genuine collectibles, making authenticity an important consideration.

  • Misrepresented Editions & Printings

    First Edition, Unlimited, regional variants, and other printing differences can affect how a card is identified and collected.

  • Altered Holographic Surfaces

    Scratches, whitening, and other surface imperfections may be concealed or manipulated to make a card appear to be in better condition.

  • Fake Slabs & Cloned Certifications

    Counterfeit holders and copied certification information can make a card appear more credible than it really is.

Counterfeit Concerns

Sports Collectors

Basketball, Football, Baseball, etc.

  • Trimmed Edges & Altered Corners

    Cards may be cut or modified to make their condition appear better than it really is.

  • Recolored Parallels & Manipulated Details

    A common card can be altered to resemble a rarer, more desirable version.

  • Counterfeit Cards & Swapped Slabs

    A convincing holder or certification label doesn't always tell the full story.

  • Unexplained Or Inconsistent Grades

    When collectors can't see how a grade was determined, they're left with questions about the result.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what collectors ask most.

Can HGA detect trimmed or altered sports cards?

HGA's software-assisted inspection is designed to help identify potential alterations, including trimming. Detection is not guaranteed, and suspicious cards may require additional review.

Does HGA authenticate autographs?

HGA does not authenticate signatures added to cards after manufacture. Collectors should not interpret a card grade as authentication of an aftermarket autograph.

How does HGA help protect against slab tampering?

HGA uses protective casing and RFID-enabled verification to provide physical and digital safeguards. These features are not a guarantee against all counterfeiting or tampering.

What is blind grading?

HGA's proposed blind-grading process is designed to limit the influence of submitter identity during grading.

What comes with each grade?

Four subgrades, a detailed grading report, protective casing, RFID technology, and access to the grading calendar. Poly and Glass + Metal are separate casing options at different prices.
