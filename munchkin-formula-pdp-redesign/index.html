Questions parents ask

Answered in plain language.

How do I switch my baby to a new formula safely?
Gradually, over about two weeks, following the schedule above: start 1 to 3, move to half and half, then 3 to 1, then full Munchkin. Most babies switch without difficulty. Watch her cues, and text the feeding line with anything that worries you.
What if it does not work for us?
Then it costs you nothing. If her stomach has not settled within 30 days of your trial, you get 100% of your money back, no return shipping, keep or donate the cans. We are the only premium formula that offers this. MBG policy
Why is there palm oil and soy on the panel?
The palm derived piece builds a fat structure (beta palmitate) that babies also get from breastmilk, and it is tied to softer stools in the research. The soy lecithin blends the oils evenly. Nothing on the panel is filler, and we will always tell you why an ingredient is there.
Why might I notice colored residue in the bottle after feeding?
The iron in formula can oxidize and leave a brownish tint once a prepared bottle sits, which is why we say feed within one hour and rinse right after. It washes with warm soapy water. If you see anything that does not match this description, text us a photo and we will look at it with you.
How do I prepare a bottle?
One unpacked level scoop per 1 fl oz of water, cap and shake until dissolved, feed within an hour. The scoop sits in its own holder at the top of the can, and the ratio is printed on the label.
Where is it made?
In one purpose built facility in Canada, inspected by both US and Canadian regulators, so every can is traceable to the same standard.
