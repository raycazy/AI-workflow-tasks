Latitude Maldives 2026

Fushifaru Maldives, Lhaviyani Atoll
1 to 4 October 2026

Relationships before business.

Latitude is a private gathering, held once a year, in a place chosen for its distance as much as its beauty. There is no stage, no exhibition booth, and nothing to walk past. A few days, a small number of people, and time enough to talk properly.

Everyone present is there by invitation. We select each designer and each brand partner with care, so that every conversation is worth the days it takes to have it. Nothing at Latitude is pitched or presented. Ideas are discussed across a table, between two people who chose to sit down together.

What forms over those days is not a lead or a contact but an acquaintance: dinners shared, water crossed, mornings without a schedule. The work follows from the relationship, in its own time, and lasts as long as the relationship does.

Trust is built in person, slowly. Everything else follows.
