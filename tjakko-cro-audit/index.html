Methodology
How this audit was built
Every finding is grounded in GA4 data, live page behaviour, or the customer's own words. Absolute GA4 numbers are directional (consent, bots and the Shopify Web Pixels Manager suppress some events), but the step to step drop-off ratios are unaffected and they drive every priority in this deck.

02
Page by page
The conversion walk
Why 96% of viewers do not add to cart. Homepage to collection to PDP to cart, every claim verified against a captured screenshot.
Homepage
A strong story, led by the wrong slide
The homepage holds attention (16.9% bounce, 4m54s on site), so the raw material is good. The issue is what gets the spotlight.
Live homepage, mobile
See the homepage redesign ▸
What we observed
  • The hero is a rotating carousel led by a SHAPEN supplier banner, not TJÅKKO's own promise. (Shapen IS the top-selling brand, so feature the products, not a generic promo.)
  • Bestseller tiles show prices but almost no star ratings, despite a 4.3/5 Trustpilot score existing.
  • A large blank mid-page gap on cold load, sections below the fold had not rendered (ties to the speed issue).
  • The "Vilken storlek ska jag ha?" sizing answer is buried in a footer FAQ accordion.
  • A prominent Shapen delay notice is the first thing a visitor reads, on the brand that drives the most revenue.
What to do
  • Lead with TJÅKKO's own benefit hero ("frihet för fötterna"), supplier promos below.
  • Add star ratings to bestseller and collection cards once reviews are surfaced.
  • Pull a "Hitta rätt storlek" entry point up near the hero.
  • Fix the render gap so the homepage paints top to bottom on mobile.
  • Scope the delay banner to affected products only.
Customers describe the product as "att sätta fötterna fria" (setting the feet free). That line belongs in the hero. A supplier's summer promo does not.
Collection / PLP
Products pushed below a wall of copy
/collections/barfotaskor. Filters exist (good), but a shopper works to reach a product, and on a slow connection may not see one at all.
Live collection, mobile
What we observed
  • On mobile the page opens with a full screen lifestyle image plus several paragraphs of barefoot education before the first product.
  • A left filter rail is present on desktop (category, size, colour), a real strength versus a plain Shopify grid.
  • On cold capture the product grid rendered blank (14,000px of mostly empty page), images load late.
  • No star ratings on any of the 64 product cards, a theme rule hides empty review badges.
  • No "for wide feet" or fit based filter, even though width is a high intent buyer question.
What to do
  • Compress the editorial intro on mobile to one line + "read more", show products in the first scroll.
  • Lazy load product images with priority hints so the grid paints fast.
  • Seed and surface review stars on cards.
  • Add a width / fit filter ("smal / normal / bred").
  • Keep the education, move it after the first product row.
A collection page exists to show products. Right now a mobile visitor reads a brand essay first, then waits for images. Both are fixable without losing the education TJÅKKO rightly cares about.
Product page
A rich PDP with an empty review box
This is where the 96% leak happens. The PDP is content rich (gallery, fit indicator, trust badges, cross-sell), but two gaps undercut it, and both map to what customers complain about.
Live product page, mobile
See the PDP redesign ▸
Working well (keep)
  • A "BRED & RYMLIG" fit indicator with a footprint diagram.
  • Trust badges (QR returns, secure delivery) and a "Storlekstabell" link.
  • A curated "Nöjda kunder" testimonial strip with photos lower down.
  • Accessory cross-sell ("Smycka skorna").
Hurting conversion (fix)
  • The Judge.me widget literally shows "0 recensioner. Inga recensioner än" right above the curated strip. An empty box reads as "no one has bought this".
  • Sizing guidance is a link to a separate table, not an inline measure guide, while sizing is the #1 review objection.
  • The size selector pre-selects the smallest size (19), inviting a wrong-size add.
  • No provenance / guarantee block to answer the "found it cheaper on Temu" doubt.
The highest impact fix in this audit: import the 42 Trustpilot reviews (4.3/5) into the on-site widget and surface the aggregate score on the PDP and collection cards. The proof already exists. It just is not on the page where 96% of viewers walk away.
Cart & checkout
No nudge toward the free shipping line
Checkout converts well (66% of starts), so this is about AOV, not fixing a broken checkout. The cart leaves the biggest AOV lever on the table.
Live cart, mobile
See the cart redesign ▸
What we observed
  • No free shipping progress bar. AOV ~1,986 SEK sits just over a 1700 kr threshold, so many carts are just under it with no nudge to add more.
  • The 1700 kr threshold is high versus rivals (simplybarefoot 498 kr).
  • No cart cross-sell beyond "recently viewed".
  • The consent checkbox renders in English: "I agree to the terms and conditions" on a Swedish store.
  • Nice touch: a sustainability opt-in ("återbrukat emballage") that fits the outdoor brand.
What to do
  • Add a progress bar: "Du är X kr från fri frakt" with a dynamic fill.
  • Add 2 to 3 low price cross-sells (socks, insoles, care) to bridge the gap.
  • Consider lowering or tiering the free shipping threshold and test it.
  • Translate the terms checkbox to Swedish.
A progress bar plus a few cross-sells is the cleanest AOV win, and it softens a 1700 kr threshold that currently reads as a barrier rather than a goal.

Steal these
Seven patterns worth adopting
Each one observed live on a named competitor, mapped to a TJÅKKO gap on the View to ATC step.
// 01
Downloadable PDF foot template
simplybarefoot ships "Ladda ner vår fotmall som PDF". A printable true-scale template kills the buy-too-small fear. TJÅKKO shows only a cm table.
// 02
Per-brand size guides
freefoot links a brand-specific guide from each PDP. TJÅKKO is multi-brand (Shapen, Be Lenka, Saltic) where lasts differ. One generic chart under-serves it.
// 03
Inline heel-to-wall method
Put the actual "paper against the wall, heel to wall" steps on the PDP, not buried on a separate page.
// 04
Plain-language buffer rule
barfooties' "+1.2 cm" rule answers "which size do I order?". Make TJÅKKO's "1 cm longer" an illustrated callout, not fine print.
// 05
Visible review proof
barfooties shows 86 reviews at 4.9/5 with snippets on the PDP. TJÅKKO surfaces none despite owning 42.
// 06
Risk reversal as a feature
groundies' "14 day test guarantee" turns returns into confidence. Elevate TJÅKKO's 40-day exchange near the ATC.
// 07 · Local trust + shipping ladder: simplybarefoot's "SVENSK BUTIK" badge and Swish build Swedish-buyer confidence, and its 498 kr threshold makes free shipping feel attainable. Add a local-trust signal and a progress-to-free-shipping bar so TJÅKKO's 1700 kr reads as a goal, not a wall.

Observation

GA4 funnel: only 3.9% of product viewers add to cart (96% drop). The PDP renders "0 recensioner" despite 42 Trustpilot reviews at 4.3/5, sizing help is a link to a separate table, and the smallest size is pre-selected.

Hypothesis

If we surface the 42 reviews and move sizing guidance inline on the PDP, then View to Add-to-Cart will rise, because the two biggest objections in customer reviews (unproven trust and sizing uncertainty) get answered at the decision point.

  • Inline foot-measure guide, per-brand size table, PDF template
  • Remove the pre-selected smallest size
