Pre sale and passes

What exactly am I buying?

A pass: prepaid grading for one card at our 15 business day tier, in the housing you choose. Every subgrade, the Grade Report and the case are included. Your passes live on your account.

When does the pre sale run?

From November 1, 2026 to January 31, 2027. The pre sale price ends when the pre sale does, and it does not come back.

How do I use a pass?

From February 1 the grading calendar opens. Reserve an open day up to a year ahead, assign one pass per card, and ship your cards to arrive that day. Each day has a capacity; a full day grays out.

Do I have to book right away? Do passes expire?

No, and no. A pass holds its value until you use it, and you choose the day: before a product release, after a big game, ahead of a show, whenever suits you.

How many passes can I buy?

Up to 100 per housing per order, and there is no limit on the number of orders.

Can I mix Poly and Glass in one order?

Yes. Add each housing to your cart; they check out together.

Can I give a pass as a gift?

Yes. Mark your order as a gift at checkout, or send any unused pass from My Passes. They accept by email, and it's theirs. The pre sale runs through the holidays, and a pass never expires.

Can I transfer a pass to another account?

Yes. Any unused pass can be sent to another account by email from My Passes.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. If you change your mind, transfer the pass to another account from My Passes.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Stripe card payments, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Sales tax is added at checkout from your billing address.

What about slower, cheaper tiers?

February 1 is our full launch. Standard turnaround tiers and their prices publish before then. The pre sale sells one thing: the 15 business day tier, at a price that does not come back.

Pricing and fees

What does a card cost to grade?

During the pre sale: $50 a card in HGA Poly, $200 in HGA Glass, for 15 business day grading. At open the same tier is $200 and $600.

Are there any extra fees?

For any card worth under $10,000, no. The pass covers grading, all subgrades, the Grade Report, autograph grading, the case, the NFT chip and live tracking. You pay for shipping when you book your day.

What about cards over $10,000?

There is no upcharge up to $10,000 in card value. Above that, insurance handling is $200 per $10,000 of value, paid when you reserve. The pass still covers the grade.

Who pays for shipping?

You choose the shipping, both directions, and pay for it at checkout when you reserve your grading day.

What happens if you miss 15 business days?

The full price of the pass is credited back to your account automatically.

Is the autograph graded at extra cost?

No. Every autograph is graded free. A 10 goes on the label; anything less stays in your Grade Report.

What is Club U pricing?

Club U members pay the member price on every tier. HGA 15 at open is $200 Poly and $600 Glass for members, $240 and $720 standard.

Does my card's value change the grading price?

No. Prices follow the turnaround, not your card's value. The only value based cost is insurance handling for cards over $10,000.

Grading and standards

Who grades my card?

An HGA grader, blind: the grader never knows whose card it is. Every card is graded to HGA's published standard and released by a senior grader.

What is the HGA grading scale?

Whole numbers from 1 to 10, plus HGA Flawless above Gem Mint 10, and Authentic for real, unaltered cards that can't be graded. No half points on the card grade.

What are the four subgrades?

Centering, corners, edges and surface. Each is scored on its own and printed on the back of every label.

How is centering measured?

With a digital centering tool on the front and back, confirmed by hand. Centering can cap a card's grade. The full front and back thresholds are on the Grading Standards page.

What does Flawless mean?

A perfect 10 on every subgrade. It earns the gold Flawless label and the Flawless Reveal. In Poly, the card moves to HGA Glass at no charge.

Do you grade altered cards?

No. Altered or counterfeit cards are never graded or cased. Any sign of trimming, recoloring, repair or cleaning sends the card to our authentication team first.

Can I appeal a grade?

There is no formal appeal. Support is built into your portal, and you can ask about any grade from the card itself.

Is the full standard public?

Yes, in full, on the Grading Standards page. The only part that stays server side is the math that combines the four subgrades into the card grade, so it can't be gamed.

Housings and card sizes

What is the difference between Poly and Glass?

Same grading, same report, same guarantee. HGA Poly is a clear case tooled to every card size. HGA Glass is a patented metal and glass housing, a display piece for premium cards.

What can I send?

Any sports card or TCG card, any size. HGA Poly has a case for every size on the market, vintage to booklets, and if yours doesn't exist yet, we make it. HGA Glass is offered for every size too.

Do you grade booklet cards?

Yes. HGA Poly is tooled for booklets, and HGA will be the first to grade and slab a six panel booklet as a single card.

My card is an unusual size. Will it fit?

Poly is tooled to 60+ sizes and chosen automatically to the nearest size that clears your card. If no case exists for your format, we pay to have one made, at no extra fee.

What is the chip in the label?

An NFT chip that ties the slab to its permanent record. Tap the label with your phone and the record opens: grade, subgrades, images and the Grade Report. A swapped label or case won't match its record.

Shipping and tracking

When does the 15 business day clock start?

When the carrier delivers your cards to HGA. It ends when your grade is revealed in your portal. Business days follow the US federal holiday calendar.

Can I see my cards while they're with HGA?

Yes. Your portal tracks every step live: a photo of your package on arrival, a video of us opening it and removing your cards, high resolution images of each card on arrival, and final images of each graded slab.

What if my card is lost or damaged?

If a card is lost or damaged in our care, we replace it or pay it out. Every time. Every step is documented in your portal.

What happens after the Reveal?

Within 7 days, choose to ship your slabs home, list them, or vault them.

Does the clock ever pause?

Only while we're waiting on you.

Your grade and report

What is the Grade Report?

The full record behind your grade: every defect a grader found, where it is on the card, how severe, with images. It is public on the cert.

What is the Reveal?

The moment you turn each slab over live in your portal and see the grade, the subgrades and the Grade Report.

How do I verify a slab?

Enter the cert number from the label, or tap the NFT chip with your phone. Compare the card in front of you with the record's images.

Is my grade public?

The cert record and the Grade Report are public on the cert, so anyone can check an HGA slab against its record.

Legacy HGA

Can I look up my legacy HGA slab?

Yes. Enter the cert number from your label on the Legacy HGA page.

Can I move a legacy slab into the new housings?

Yes. Legacy HGA slabs can come back for recertification to the new standard and the new housings. Pricing publishes before recertification opens.

Will legacy grades appear in the population report?

Legacy HGA slabs join the report as they are recertified to the current standard.

Can I cross over a slab from another company?

Yes. Other companies' slabs cross over the same way: recertified to the HGA standard and rehoused.

About HGA

Who is behind HGA?

HGA is a uCollect company, based in Chandler, Arizona. uCollect owns HGA; HGA grades independently, to its published standard, and does not buy, sell or own cards.

What does Hybrid Grading Approach mean?

Expert graders working to a published standard, with software recording every finding. People make the calls; the record makes them visible.

What changed at HGA?

HGA had real problems in the past: turnaround times, housings and more. We rebuilt it around a guaranteed turnaround, a published standard, new housings and a full report on every card.
