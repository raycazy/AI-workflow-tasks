Logo

logo usage

the lowercase serif "sitre" wordmark is the primary asset. keep clear space around it equal to the height of the mark, and only place it on the approved backgrounds below.

the wordmark renders solid black on light surfaces and reverses to white on deep green. the green mark is used as the favicon.

do
keep clear space equal to the cap height. use black on light surfaces, white on deep green. use the green mark only at small favicon sizes.
do not
recolor the wordmark to olive or beige. do not add effects, outlines, or shadows. do not place black on a busy photo without a scrim.
