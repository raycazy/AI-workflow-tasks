The Cazyweb design system.

A cool, neutral, glass-forward theme: clean whites, calm slate ink, and a single blue accent. Every color, type style, and component on this page reads from one token block so anything we design stays on brand.

Theme: Cazyweb new Tone: cool neutral + blue Material: Liquid Glass
Brand essence

Calm, clean, a little bit blue.

The visual system is cool and neutral (white, slate ink, cool gray) lifted by a single blue accent. Type is almost entirely light weight, everything is generously rounded, and surfaces use a glass material so content shines through. Quiet by default, with one clear pop of color for action.

It is
  • Cool, neutral whites with a blue pop
  • Light-weight, oversized headlines
  • Very rounded: pill buttons, 48px cards
  • Glass surfaces, content shines through
It is not
  • Warm beige or heavy color
  • Heavy, bold, condensed type
  • Sharp corners or hard edges
  • Loud, saturated, or busy

Logo

Logo usage

The Cazyweb logo is the primary asset: the rounded "c" mark plus the lowercase wordmark, in a single color. Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the mark, and only place it on the approved backgrounds below.

Single color only. Render in black on light surfaces and white (knockout) on dark or blue. Never recolor or add effects to the mark.

Do
Keep it lowercase. Give it room. Use a single brand color.
Do not
Add gradients or outlines. Stretch it. Place it on a busy photo without a scrim.

System

Liquid Glass

Cazyweb's surfaces use a Liquid Glass material: Apple's dynamic material that "combines the optical properties of glass with a sense of fluidity," letting the content behind it shine through and infuse the surface. We tint it cool so it stays on brand. Two variants: Regular (more opaque, adaptive, stays legible) for chat, nav, and most controls; Clear (more transparent) for media-rich moments.

Regular and Clear, over photography

Regular

Cool tint, adaptive, stays legible. Use for chat bubbles, navigation, toolbars, and most controls.

Clear

More transparent, lets the imagery dominate. Use over rich media and full bleed moments.

Controls, over a moody gradient

Material recipe (regular)

/* translucent warm tint + backdrop blur:
   content infuses the glass */
.glass {
  position: relative;
  isolation: isolate;
  overflow: hidden;
  border-radius: 26px;            /* continuous radius */
}
.glass::before {                  /* the glass body */
  content: ""; position: absolute; inset: 0;
  z-index: -2; border-radius: inherit;
  background: rgba(244,247,251,.40);
  backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(185%) brightness(1.06);
  box-shadow:
    inset 0 1.5px 1px rgba(255,255,255,.7),   /* top light */
    inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,.32);    /* lens rim */
}
.glass::after {                   /* specular sheen */
  content: ""; position: absolute; inset: 0;
  z-index: -1; border-radius: inherit;
  background: linear-gradient(130deg,
    rgba(255,255,255,.5) 0%, transparent 34%);
}
/* float above content */
box-shadow: 0 18px 44px rgba(31,37,51,.28);

Properties

  • Translucency: content shines through and infuses the surface
  • Refraction: backdrop blur with boosted saturation acts as a lens
  • Specular: a bright inset top light and a diagonal sheen
  • Lens rim: a hairline white edge defines the glass
  • Float: a soft shadow separates it from content
  • Shape: continuous large radii, capsule controls

Source: Apple, Liquid Glass (developer documentation). Be judicious with color so content stays legible and shines through.

Voice & copy

We write as one team: confident, direct, slightly bold. The "Excellence is not optional." register, founder direct, not corporate polished. Short sentences, active voice, present tense. We lead with evidence, not adjectives, and every claim has to survive the question "show me where that came from."

"Excellence is not optional."
The register
"We build what's next."
Always "we", never "I"
"+140% sales beats incredible results."
Specific over superlative
"DTC kitchenware brand, US."
NDA-safe by niche plus scale
Do
  • Say "we", never "I"
  • Lead with real numbers and named outcomes
  • State prices plainly ("From $8k/mo")
  • Make CTAs verb plus object ("Book a Partner Call")
  • Declare the audience: For Brands or For Agencies
Do not
  • Invent names, stats, or stock team members
  • Use filler: cutting-edge, unlock, elevate, world-class, seamless
  • Use em dashes or en dashes (plain hyphens only)
  • Say "contact us for pricing" or "Learn More"
  • Talk about our ambition over the buyer's risk
