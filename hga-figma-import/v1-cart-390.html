Do I need to ship my cards now?

No. You're buying passes today. From February 1, reserve a grading day up to a year ahead and ship your cards to arrive that day.

Can I split passes across different cards and days?

Yes. One pass grades one card. Use them across as many grading days as you like.

Can I buy these as a gift?

Yes. Mark the order as a gift at checkout and we'll email the passes to them, or transfer any unused pass later from My Passes.
