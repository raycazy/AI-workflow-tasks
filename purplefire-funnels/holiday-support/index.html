Unlimited pages for brands behind on Black Friday

You offer past clients unlimited landing pages plus website design and development support from now to the end of December, for one flat fee.

The idea

Many brand teams are behind on their Black Friday, Cyber Monday and holiday pages this week, and every new page waits in the same queue as the ads and the emails. You offer them hands for the season: as many landing pages and site changes as they need, built by PurpleFire until Thu 31 Dec 2026. You come in as the people who know holiday sale and offer strategy from 5,000+ A/B tests and 1,300+ documented winners, so each page starts from what you have seen win.

Hypothesis

A team that is behind on Black Friday will say yes to extra hands this week more easily than to a strategy call. It is wrong if no brand on the list says yes to the offer or to a 15 minute call by Fri 4 Dec 2026.

The offer

Unlimited means
The brand sends as many requests as it likes. They go in one at a time, and each one is done before the next one starts.
What is covered
Landing pages for the sale, offer and bundle pages, gift guide pages, announcement and countdown bars, cart offers, checkout offers where their Shopify plan allows them, the Cyber Monday pages, shipping cutoff banners, the January sale page (built in December) and fixes on the live theme.
The window
From the day they say yes to Thu 31 Dec 2026.

Brands at a time

Assets

What to cover on the 15 minute call. These are pointers for you, not a script.

Five questions to ask

  1. What is your offer for Black Friday, and does it change for Cyber Monday?
  2. What is already built, and what is not built yet?
  3. Who builds your pages today, and how much room do they have left this month?
  4. When is your code freeze?
  5. What happens on the site after Cyber Monday, through December and into January?

What can still go live before Fri 27 Nov

If they start by Wed 18 Nov 2026, there are about seven working days before Black Friday. Requests go one at a time, so a few of these fit, not all of them. Agree on the call which come first:

  • A new sale landing page
  • Offer and bundle pages built from products they already sell
  • Announcement and countdown bars
  • Shipping cutoff banners
  • Cart offers, and checkout offers where their Shopify plan allows them
  • The Cyber Monday pages, ready to switch on
  • Small fixes on the live theme

What moves to December

  • A full redesign or a new theme: not part of this offer, a separate conversation for January
  • Changes across every template on the site
  • Gift guide pages for the holiday weeks and the January sale page

A yes on Thu 19 or Fri 20 Nov leaves room for two or three of these Est., so start with the one that matters most. US teams are mostly off on Thu 26 Nov, so plan for everything to be live by Wed 25 Nov. Anything that falls after their code freeze waits until the freeze lifts.

Holiday sale and offer pointers

Things you can speak to when they describe their offer. Pick the two or three that fit what you hear.

  • Tiered offers: a bigger reward at two or three spend levels, so the offer asks for a bigger basket instead of only a lower price.
  • Gift with purchase: a gift above a spend level, for brands whose margin cannot take a deeper discount.
  • Bundles: ready made sets at one price, which also work as gift ideas.
  • Deadlines and shipping cutoffs: one clear end time for each offer, and the last day to order for delivery before Christmas, shown in the bar, on the product page and in the cart.
  • The Cyber Monday swap: pages, bars and banners for the Cyber Monday offer ready before Black Friday, so the switch on Mon 30 Nov is a planned change and not a late night edit on the live theme.
  • Gift guides: pages sorted by who the gift is for or by price, linked from the menu and the bar in the December weeks.
  • January sale: planned now, so the pages after the holidays are not built in a rush.

If they ask what this is based on: 5,000+ A/B tests and 1,300+ documented winners. Do not quote a result from any single client on this call.

What they give you on day one

  • A staff or collaborator account on the store
  • Brand assets: logo, fonts, product photos and any campaign images
  • The offer and its dates, for Black Friday and for Cyber Monday
  • One person who approves each page

After the season

Every page built in the season is a page you can A/B test in January, so a brand that says yes now is the natural first conversation about January testing.
