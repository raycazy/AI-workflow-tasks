All questions
Do I have to book right away? Do passes expire?

No, and no. A pass holds its value until you use it, and you choose the day: before a release, after a big game, ahead of a show.

Can I mix Poly and Glass?

Yes. Add each housing to your cart separately; they check out together. Up to 100 per housing per order, and you can place as many orders as you like.

What can I send?

Any sports card or TCG card, any size. HGA Poly has a case for every size on the market, vintage to booklets, and if yours doesn't exist yet, we make it.

Can I transfer or gift a slot?

No. Pre-launch purchases are tied to the purchasing account and cannot be transferred.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final, and pre-launch purchases cannot be transferred.
