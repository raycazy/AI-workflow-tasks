Past clients get a check of their own winners

Plus two passes for founder friends. Nothing is hosted. The AI check starts Mon 16 Nov once you approve the kit, and you finish your contact sheet by Wed 25 Nov; see Run it.

What changes

Today

Every funnel Daniel runs buys cold attention, and his past clients appear only as logos and proof cards.

New

Past client founders get, as the first message, a check of which of their own winners are still live, made by AI from the winners list and signed by Daniel, with two passes for founder friends. Nothing is asked of them before it arrives.

The test

Who it is for
The founders and teams behind the vault cards, and the people and friends they lead to.
Hypothesis
People who already saw his tests win produce qualified calls (a restart, an intro or a used pass) at a rate cold traffic has not; fewer than 3 qualified calls by Fri 18 Dec proves it false.
Segments
Read by source tag (no arms; a directional read on tens of contacts): A, past client founders and team, who each get the check; B, job changers now at another brand, who get a note that asks for nothing; C, founder friends reached through a pass or an agreed intro.
Keep
At least 3 qualified calls by Fri 18 Dec.
Kill
Fewer than 3 replies asking to talk from the first 30 checks by Fri 11 Dec. If fewer than 30 checks go out, the read covers all of them that day.
Cost
Est. Media $0, optional video about $255. Your reading time is about 5 to 10 minutes per check before you sign it (Est.), longer for a brand with many winners.
Passes
A client's results appear on a pass only after that client says they can. By default a pass carries the offer only.
Shares
Kit 02's page (where passes land) and its calendars; the vault cards; one GHL template.

Risks

  • A "not found" read as blame.
  • A wrong "not found" in a check nobody asked for. Arriving unasked, one wrong mark costs more trust than it would in a check the client asked for, so you read and sign every check, and a winner whose page cannot be judged from outside (a cart or checkout step, or a change shown to one device only) is marked Not checked, never guessed.
  • Old winners mostly changed. The first AI pass shows this by Fri 20 Nov, before anything is sent.
  • A reachable list far below the 71 past clients in the vault, so this runs beside the ad funnel.

Before anything is sent

  • You confirm, per client, the brand name they knew you under and that no work was done under another agency.

Nothing goes to a Danish contact until Daniel names his countries and a legal read confirms the format.
