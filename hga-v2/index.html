All 48 answers
What is a pre-launch slot?

Prepaid grading for one card at the 15 business day tier, in the housing you choose, at pre-launch pricing. Your slots live on your account. Up to 100 per housing per order, with no limit on orders.

When can I send my cards?

HGA officially reopens February 1, 2027. From then, reserve a grading day up to a year ahead and ship your cards to arrive that day. The 15 business day clock starts at carrier delivery.

What is Glass Box Grading™?

HGA's new grading experience: software assisted grading, card tracking, detailed grading reasons, digital verification and a guaranteed turnaround, all visible to you through uCollect.

What can I send?

Any sports card or TCG card, any size. HGA Poly has a case for every size on the market, vintage to booklets, and if yours doesn't exist yet, we make it. HGA Glass is offered for every size too.

What about cards worth over $10,000?

There is no card value upcharge under $10,000. For cards valued above $10,000, a 2% insurance and handling upcharge applies, paid when you reserve. The slot still covers the grade.

Can I transfer or gift a pre-launch slot?

No. Pre-launch purchases are tied to the purchasing account and cannot be transferred.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final, and pre-launch purchases cannot be transferred.
