Know everything.

  1. Blind grading for greater objectivity

    HGA separates centering and grading into distinct steps, while keeping graders unaware of whose card they're evaluating. The focus stays on the card, not its owner.

  2. Guaranteed delivery to reveal clock

    Know exactly when your grade is due, backed by HGA's 15 business day "no excuses" guarantee.

  3. Software assisted centering

    Software works from high resolution images to assist on the centering grade. Corners, edges and surface are graded by a human.

  4. Autograph grading included

    Submitting a signed card? Get an autograph grade where applicable without paying for an additional add on.

  5. RFID verification and blockchain certification

    Tap your card's label with your phone to access its permanent digital record. Each card is blockchain certified, an additional layer of protection against card swapping and fraudulent verification.

  6. No card value upcharge under $10,000

    Pay the same grading price on cards valued under $10,000, without surprise value based fees. Above $10,000, a 2% insurance and handling upcharge applies.

Questions

Before you join.

All answers
Is this a presale? When do I send my cards?

Yes, this is a presale. HGA goes live February 1, 2027, and that is when you send your cards in: reserve a grading day up to a year ahead and ship your cards to arrive that day. Presale pricing ends when we go live. The 15 business day clock starts at carrier delivery.

What is the HGA Grading Calendar?

It's how you book your grade. Pick the day you'll send in your card, and we reserve grading capacity for that date before it ships. We plan capacity up front instead of guessing at a turnaround, so you know what to expect and we deliver on time. The calendar opens February 1, 2027 for you to use your pre-sale passes to schedule your submission.

What is a pre-launch slot?

Prepaid grading for one card at the 15 business day tier, in the housing you choose, at pre-launch pricing. Your slots live on your account. Up to 100 per housing per order, with no limit on orders.

What is Glass Box Grading™?

HGA's new grading experience: human grading with software assisted centering, card tracking, detailed grading reasons, digital verification and a guaranteed turnaround, all visible to you.

What can I send?

Any sports card or TCG card, any size. HGA Poly has a case for every size on the market, vintage to booklets, and if yours doesn't exist yet, we make it. HGA Glass is offered for every size too.

What will grading cost after launch?

Pricing follows the housing and the turnaround, never your card's value. At launch, HGA Poly runs from $29 (Bulk, 60 business days) to $150 (15 business days), and HGA Glass from $100 to $600. The presale locks in 15 business day grading at $29 and $200. See the full price list.

What about cards worth over $10,000?

There is no card value upcharge under $10,000. For cards valued above $10,000, a 2% insurance and handling upcharge applies, paid when you reserve. The slot still covers the grade.

Can I transfer or gift a pre-launch slot?

No. Pre-launch purchases are tied to the purchasing account and cannot be transferred.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final, and pre-launch purchases cannot be transferred.
