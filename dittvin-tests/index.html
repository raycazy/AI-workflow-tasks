What changed in the variant

1Honest delivery block with a 2 to 5 day range and a promise you can pick the day. Replaces the "1-3 dagar" claim that drives 86% of complaints.
2Free shipping made unambiguous. The Airmee condition is stated up front and repeated at the total, so nobody is surprised by a Bring fee later.
3Trust at the moment of payment: tax and VAT included, Klarna and card marks, "trygg betalning". Neutralises the legitimacy doubt right before checkout.
4Upsell reframed as free shipping progress ("lägg till och nå fri frakt") instead of a generic "du kanske gillar", giving the add on a reason.
