TL;DR

The five biggest wins, ranked.

Implemented together these five changes address the hero, social proof, payment friction, product discovery, and the buy box. Each one is a known lift driver for premium ecommerce in Denmark. None require a replatform.

01 · HERO
Sell the store, not a single Saturday.
The fold is occupied by a one day family event. New visitors get no signal this is a fitness equipment store. Rebuild around categories and the brand promise.
02 · REVIEWS
Promote the 4,5 stars you already have.
Trustpilot says "Fremragende". The site says nothing. Add star ratings to product cards, a reviews block on every PDP, and a Trustbox under the hero.
03 · FINANCING
"Fra 1.158 kr / md" reframes 13.900 kr.
Zero pay later option on PDP. Klarna and ViaBill are standard in Denmark above 2.000 kr. Add the monthly equivalent right under the price.
04 · FILTERS
Treadmills from 4k to 40k. No way to narrow.
The Løbebånd page filters by stock and price. Add brand, motor power, max user weight, foldable, smart app, and incline. Apply across all major categories.
05 · BUY BOX
Rebuild the PDP for premium products.
Spec chips above the fold. Discount badge, trust badges, financing, demo video. Swap competing cross sells for accessories that finish the setup.

The 5 Conversion Blockers

Ranked by leverage, not by effort.

Each blocker is sourced from the live site, cross checked with Danish competitors (Fitshop, Fitness Engros, Træningspartner), and the brand's own Trustpilot record. Where we say "missing", we have screenshot evidence.

01
Critical Homepage · Above the fold Desktop & mobile

The hero sells one Saturday, not a fitness store.

A first time visitor lands on a busy red banner for a family event on 31. maj. The store's value proposition is nowhere on the first screen.

What we see

On desktop, the left half of the hero is a packed promotional graphic for "KOM TIL EN SJOV DAG FOR HELE FAMILIEN" with gokart, popcorn, slushie, and trampoline tiles. The right half repeats the event details: 31. maj, 10:00 to 15:00, Sindalsvej 38. On mobile, the entire first viewport is the event banner. The CTA "Læg i indkøbskurv" never appears. No category is named, no product is shown, no headline explains what the site sells.

Why it hurts

The header navigation does the heavy lifting (Kondition, Styrke, Trampoliner, etc.), but on mobile, where most consumer ecom sessions happen, the nav is collapsed behind a hamburger. The hero is the single highest impression area on the site. Spending it on a 6 hour event that expires on 1. juni means:

  • New visitors arriving from Google for "løbebånd" see no path to løbebånd.
  • From 1. juni the hero is selling an expired event.
  • The brand promise ("Alt til et aktivt liv, samlet ét sted") never appears.
  • Paid traffic landing on the home with no category intent gets nothing relevant.

The fix

  • Compress the event to a thin announcement strip below the existing "Gratis fragt" bar. Keep the hype, lose the real estate.
  • Replace the hero with a category led layout: lifestyle visual, brand promise headline, three to six tile shortcuts (Løbebånd, Romaskine, Smart Home Gym, Trampoliner, BERG, Tilbud).
  • Surface the four trust USPs (gratis fragt 499 kr, 365 dages retur, hurtig levering fra eget lager, showroom i Aarhus) as a strip directly under the hero, visible without scrolling.
  • Add a secondary CTA "Find det rigtige løbebånd" leading to a guided buying flow.
GA4 evidence

Homepage as a landing page converts at 2,26 % (6 / 265 sessions). The top three PDP landing pages convert at 0,18 to 0,31 %. The homepage out-converts every paid PDP landing page on the site, even with the event hero. Imagine if it actually sold the category.

02
Critical Sitewide Trust & social proof

You have 4,5 stars. Nobody on the site knows.

Trustpilot rates SportGym ApS at 4,5 / 5 ("Fremragende"). The homepage, the collection cards, and the PDP show zero ratings, zero counts, zero customer quotes.

What we see

On dk.trustpilot.com/review/sportgym.dk the score reads 4,5 / 5 across 15 anmeldelser. On the live site, the Trustpilot integration referenced in the source is not visible as a ratings widget on the homepage, no star ratings appear on the 13 treadmill product cards in /collections/lobebaend, and the VirtuFit TR600i PDP has no customer review section. The buyer has to leave the site to find out the brand is well rated.

Why it hurts

For products priced 4.000 to 40.000 kr, social proof is the single largest trust lever. A customer comparing your TR600i against the same model on Fitshop sees that competitor's 4,84 Google score (41.278 reviews) on screen. Yours requires a separate tab. The 4,5 Trustpilot score is an asset the store is not spending. Worse: with only 15 reviews against Træningspartner's 807 and BERG's 4.000+, there is no post purchase review request flow in place, so the asset is not growing.

The fix

  • Embed a Trustpilot Trustbox immediately below the hero on the homepage. The "Fremragende 4,5" line is your most powerful headline.
  • Install a Shopify review app (Judge.me, Stamped, or Trustpilot Product Reviews) and show star ratings on every collection product card.
  • Add a reviews block on every PDP with star average, count, verified buyer badge, and three to five most recent quotes. Pin one with photo if available.
  • Build a post purchase Klaviyo flow: review request email 14 days after delivery, follow up at 30 days. Offer a small thank you (next order discount or free accessory).
  • Print one customer quote in the order confirmation page and on the unboxing card to drive offline reviews.
GA4 evidence

2.663 users viewed a product in the last 90 days. 2.535 left without adding to cart. A 95 % drop. The PDP has zero ratings to push the undecided buyer over the edge. The "Fremragende" 4,5 score is the missing piece exactly where the funnel breaks.

03
Critical PDP & Cart Pricing & payment

No financing. On a 13.900 kr product.

A premium treadmill at the Danish standard threshold for pay later, and no Klarna, ViaBill or AnyDay anywhere on the PDP.

What we see

The TR600i PDP shows 13.900 kr (struck through 17.900 kr) with no monthly equivalent. Footer payment icons display Visa, Mastercard, MobilePay, Apple Pay. No Klarna, no ViaBill, no AnyDay, no Sparxpres. By comparison, fitshop.dk shows both Klarna and ViaBill at the page level and in checkout.

Why it hurts

Pay later is a near default expectation in Denmark above 2.000 kr. For a 13.900 kr treadmill, the absence of a monthly framing converts an "I can afford this in twelve months" buy into a "I cannot afford this today" exit. The headline price feels like the full ask. Reframing it as "Fra 1.158 kr / md med ViaBill" or "Klarna delbetaling i 24 mdr" changes the affordability conversation without dropping the price.

The fix

  • Install ViaBill or Klarna pay later via Shopify app. Both have native Danish PDP widgets.
  • Display the monthly equivalent directly under the price on every PDP above 1.500 kr.
  • Add a small block under the CTA: "Op til 12 måneder rentefri delbetaling".
  • Rotate "Delbetaling tilgængelig" into the top USP strip alongside "Gratis fragt over 499 kr".
  • Place the financing provider logo in the footer icon row and in cart and checkout.
GA4 evidence

AOV across 73 orders is 5.015 kr. Top 10 orders averaged 16.343 kr (highest 26.392 kr). Desktop AOV is 7.661 kr. These are buyers who finance major purchases. Without a monthly framing on the PDP, you ask them to commit to the full ticket upfront and lose the ones who would have said yes to 12 months.

04
High Collection page Discovery

13 treadmills from 3.495 to 39.900 kr. Two filters.

The Løbebånd page filters only by stock and price. No brand, no motor power, no max user weight, no foldable, no smart, no incline. The shopper has nothing to narrow with.

What we see

The /collections/lobebaend page lists 13 products. Available filters: Tilgængelighed (in stock yes/no) and Pris (range with max 69.900 kr). No brand toggle, no motor power, no max user weight, no max speed, no foldability, no smart connectivity, no incline. The product cards themselves show image, name, price, and a discount badge on some. No specs, no ratings, no quick view, no compare.

Why it hurts

A buyer with a real requirement (for example, walking pad for under 5.000 kr, supports 130 kg, fits under a bed) has to manually open every product to check. After three or four PDPs, they leave. A 12x price range across the same category needs structured filtering. This is the funnel's narrow stretch where most paid traffic decisions are made, and the page does almost nothing to help.

The fix

  • Use a Shopify filter app (Searchanise, Boost AI Search, or Smart Product Filter) to surface structured filters per category.
  • For Løbebånd: Brand, Motor (HP), Max brugervægt (kg), Maks hastighed (km/t), Hældning (%), Foldbart, Smart / App.
  • For Trampoliner: Størrelse, Form, Sikkerhedsnet, Aldersgruppe, Mærke.
  • For Styrke: Type (frie vægte, stativ, bænk), Max load, Materiale, Foldbart.
  • Show three to five spec chips on each product card: "3 HP · 18 km/t · 150 kg · Foldbart".
  • Add a "Sammenlign" feature for up to three products.
  • Add a "Find dit løbebånd" quiz at the top of the page: 4 questions returning a shortlist.
  • Enable sort by rating once the review widget is live.
GA4 evidence

Paid social currently bypasses the collection page entirely and lands on individual PDPs. But organic search and direct visitors hit category pages first. Collection / Kettlebell received 35 sessions, /collections/isbad got 80 sessions, /collections/trampoliner-til-nedgravning 81 sessions. These are buyers actively browsing categories with intent. Today they get two filters and no spec context.

05
High Product page Buy box

The PDP buy box is built for a 200 kr product.

SKU, name, price, qty, CTA, two collapsed accordions. That is the entire fold for a 13.900 kr purchase. The conversion stack a premium product needs is absent.

What we see

Above the fold on /products/virtufit-tr600i-lobeband: SKU "VF20313", product title, struck through 17.900 kr (small), 13.900 kr (large), qty selector, stock note "På lager 1-3 hverdages levering", CTA "Læg i indkøbskurv", pickup option, two collapsed accordions ("Gratis fragt over 499,-" and "Showroom i Aarhus"). The right column has a large empty space below the CTA. No video, no spec highlight chips, no save amount badge, no trust badges in proximity to the CTA, no financing.

Below the fold there is a strong description and a specifications table. Then "Relaterede produkter" shows four other treadmills priced 12.900, 4.995, 3.495, 7.495 kr. The cross sell competes with the current product instead of completing the setup.

Why it hurts

At this price point the buyer needs confidence (specs at a glance, trust signals, reviews), risk reduction (financing, returns proximity, warranty), demonstration (video, lifestyle shots), and comparison context. The current PDP gives them none above the fold and the cross sell module pushes them sideways to a different treadmill rather than upward to "what else do I need".

The fix · buy box rebuild

  • Discount badge: "SPAR 4.000 kr · -22%" above the price. The savings exists, surface it.
  • Financing line: "Fra 1.158 kr / md med ViaBill" directly under the price.
  • Spec chip row: 4 chips immediately under the title: "3,0 HP · 18 km/t · 150 kg · Foldbart".
  • Trust badge row: 4 icons below the CTA: 2 års garanti, Gratis fragt over 499 kr, 365 dages retur, Showroom i Aarhus.
  • Stock urgency: "Kun 3 tilbage på lageret" when inventory is low. Show "Bestil før kl. 14 for levering i morgen" when applicable.
  • Demo video: 20 to 60 second video as the second gallery item. Show the fold mechanism and the display in use.
  • Cross sell module: Rename "Relaterede produkter" to "Komplet dit setup". Show floor mat, lubricant, gulvmåtte, heart rate strap. One step up model at the end for upsell context.
  • Comparison table: This model vs TR100i and TR1500i. Three columns, eight rows of specs.
  • FAQ block: Assembly time, motor warranty, return policy for large items, financing terms, delivery for large items.
  • Sticky add to cart on mobile: The CTA is below the fold on a 390 wide viewport. Stick the price plus CTA at the bottom of the screen once the user scrolls past the gallery.
GA4 evidence · this is THE bottleneck

View product to add to cart drops 95 % overall. Mobile drops 96,2 %. Desktop drops 86,1 %. Same PDP, very different mobile experience. If the rebuilt PDP recovers even 1 percentage point on mobile (3,8 % to 4,8 % ATC rate), that's roughly 23 extra ATCs over 90 days at the current 43 % ATC to purchase rate, or about 50.000 kr in incremental revenue at the current AOV. The math gets bigger as you raise the ATC rate.
