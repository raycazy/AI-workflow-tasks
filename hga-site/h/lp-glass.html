Frequently Asked Questions

Can HGA detect trimmed or altered sports cards?

HGA's software-assisted inspection is designed to help identify potential alterations, including trimming. Detection is not guaranteed, and suspicious cards may require additional review.

Does HGA authenticate autographs?

HGA does not authenticate signatures added to cards after manufacture. Collectors should not interpret a card grade as authentication of an aftermarket autograph.

How does HGA help protect against slab tampering?

HGA uses protective casing and RFID-enabled verification to provide physical and digital safeguards. These features are not a guarantee against all counterfeiting or tampering.

What is blind grading?

HGA's proposed blind-grading process is designed to limit the influence of submitter identity during grading.

What comes with each grade?

Four subgrades, a detailed grading report, protective casing, RFID technology, and access to the grading calendar. Poly and Glass + Metal are separate casing options at different prices.
