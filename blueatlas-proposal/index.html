What we need from you

To keep the build on schedule.

A smooth project depends on a few inputs and timely sign-offs from your side. The sooner these arrive, the tighter the timeline holds.

  • One decision-maker as our single point of contact for approvals.
  • Domain and hosting access, or budget for us to procure them.
  • Real service data: visa types, countries, processing times, fees, success rates.
  • Legal and compliance text: terms, privacy, refund, any licences or accreditations.
  • Pricing to display and any promotions or packages.
  • CRM or email destination for leads (e.g. GoHighLevel, HubSpot, or a shared inbox).
  • Existing assets, if any: real testimonials, logos, photos, certifications.
  • Content approvals within 2 to 3 business days at each review.
