Quick
answers.

Do I need to ship my cards now?

No. You're buying passes today. From January 18, book a grading date and ship your cards any time before it. Grading starts February 1, so that day.

Can I split passes across different cards and days?

Yes. One pass grades one card. Use them across as many grading days as you like.

Can I buy these as a gift?

No. Pre-launch purchases are tied to the purchasing account and cannot be transferred or gifted.

What if I want a refund?

All sales are final. Pre-launch purchases cannot be transferred.
