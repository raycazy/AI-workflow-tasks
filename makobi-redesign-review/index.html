08 / PDP (the leak)

PDP: financing, sticky ATC, and size guide are the missing pieces.

New PDP (mobile)Preview
What to fixHigh priority

Kept in new theme: size chart link, stock urgency line, reviews block, ShopPay express, free-ship messaging. Good foundation. The list below is the remaining gap.

🚨
ATC upsell popup blocks the cart drawer (bug, new theme)When the upsell popup appears after Add-to-Cart: accepting opens the cart drawer instantly, but closing/rejecting either doesn't open the drawer OR opens it after a 5 to 6 second delay. Users drop in that window. Also: the popup has no explicit "Decline" button, only a close X. Fix: close popup, open drawer instantly, every path. Add an explicit "No thanks, continue" CTA.
🚨
Placeholder product copy (launch blocker)Every PDP currently shows "Details will be added soon", "Features will be added soon", "Where-to-wear information will be added soon" in the product accordions. Populate every SKU's Product Details, Style Features, and Where to Style before launch. Streetwear shoppers read this copy: it's a real ATC lever, not filler.
✗
Afterpay was removed (regression)The old PDP showed "Afterpay available for orders up to $4,000" directly under the price. The new theme dropped it. Restore Afterpay (or upgrade to Shop Pay Installments). Single highest ATC lever at $98 to $140 AOV.
✗
No sticky mobile ATC barSticky bottom bar with thumb + size + ATC needs to appear after the user scrolls past the buy box. Mobile drives 90% of real revenue.
✗
No notify-me on sold-out variantsMost PDP sizes are sold out and the new theme doesn't capture restock intent. Add a "Notify me" email capture on every sold-out variant.
✗
No model fit context"Model is 6'1", chest 42, waist 34. Wearing size 34/32" near the size selector cuts return rates on jeans.
✗
Photo reviews not surfacedReviews block shows star ratings only. Streetwear shoppers want to see real customers wearing the product. Pull the photo upload view from your existing review app.

14 / A/B test roadmap

10 tests to run
after launch.

Sequence built around the data. Test 01 is the holistic theme test (current site vs. the new theme with the recommended changes baked in). Tests 02 through 10 then optimize individual moves on the winning theme.

# Test Page Primary metric P
01 Theme test: Current site vs. new theme (with all recommended changes baked in) Holistic A/B between live makobiusa.com and the new theme with cart UX, Shop Pay Installments, sticky ATC, size guide, swatches, low-stock, and Big & Tall promotion shipped. Site-wide Mobile session CR + revenue per session P0
02 Cart UX: express checkout buttons + in-cart cross-sells + trust strip Free-ship progress bar already shipped in the new design (good). Remaining gap to close the 28% ATC to Begin-Checkout drop: surface Apple Pay / G Pay / Shop Pay buttons above the standard checkout, add "complete the look" cross-sells, and a trust strip next to the checkout CTA. Also test lowering the $500 free-ship threshold. Cart ATC to Begin Checkout rate P0
03 PDP: Restore Afterpay badge under price (regression fix) The old site showed "Afterpay available for orders up to $4,000" inline with the price. New theme removed it. Restore it, or test Shop Pay Installments / Klarna as an upgrade. PDP VP to ATC rate P0
04 PDP: Sticky mobile ATC bar Persistent buy button after the user scrolls past the buy box. Mobile drives 90% of revenue. PDP Mobile ATC rate P1
05 Home: Big & Tall in primary nav + dedicated hero block Promoting the #2 collection (102K views/90d) from a buried mega-menu item to a primary entry point. Home Big & Tall PDP entries + CR P1
06 Cart: Lower free-shipping threshold ($250 vs current $500) At $98 to $140 AOV, $500 means buying 4+ items. Lower threshold lifts AOV efficiently and closes the ATC to Checkout gap. Cart AOV, ATC to Checkout P1
07 PDP: Notify-me on sold-out variants + photo reviews surfaced Capture intent from out-of-stock interest; lift social proof with verified buyer photos above text reviews. PDP Email capture, VP to ATC P2
08 PLP: Color swatches on product cards Show colorway options on the card so customers don't open every PDP. Lifts CTR into PDP. PLP PLP to PDP CTR P2
09 PLP: Quick-add from collection grid Single-tap add for single-variant products. Shortens the funnel for repeat buyers and known SKUs. PLP PLP to ATC bypass rate P2
10 Home: Single-product editorial hero vs. category slideshow A focused single-product hero (the current concept) vs. a multi-category rotating slideshow. Home Home to PDP CTR P2

Run tests sequentially, not concurrently, on the same page area to avoid interaction effects. Minimum sample = ~2,500 mobile users per variant (based on 340K mobile sessions/90d). Expect each test to take 14 to 21 days at significance.
